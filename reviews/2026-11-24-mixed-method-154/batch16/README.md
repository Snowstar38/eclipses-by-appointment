# Four deep single-sector dip alerts

Two of these four raw TESS signals recover previously identified eclipsing systems. See the [four dispositions](outcomes.csv) and [original event measurements](archive-windows.csv).

**TIC 120239458.** Théo Chopard-Vilhem's released pilot measured the deep sector 16 eclipse at BTJD 1741.1502 and matched it to the Gaia orbit of TYC 2746-1373-1. His tables also contain the prediction for the other conjunction. We measured that shallower eclipse in the original two-minute SPOC light curve. The central losses are 17.4% and 0.99% in raw flux and 19.7% and 1.03% after pipeline correction. The two centers are 6.76222 days apart in corrected flux. The covariance of Gaia's 43.2858-day, eccentric orbit predicts a median of 6.773 days between conjunctions, with a 68% interval of 6.577–6.973 days. Both the timing and the full-frame positions support the same binary. [Two eclipses](120239458-two-eclipses.png) · [Timing fits](120239458-eclipses.csv) · [Gaia orbit and earlier observations](gaia/README.md) · [Chopard-Vilhem's pilot release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23342416)

The TESS footprint service returns sectors 16, 56 and 83. Only sector 16 provides images of this star on illuminated pixels. In the median images for the two later sectors the entire central 3-by-3 patch is zero. The original sector 16 SPOC threshold-crossing report also flagged the deep event, at an alternative period. The Villanova target page's signal row has a false catalogue-membership flag and an empty ephemeris. [Sector images](120239458-sector-coverage.png) · [Pipeline detection](../followup/dv-120239458.json) · [Villanova entry](https://tessebs.villanova.edu/0120239458)

**TIC 298262982.** The broad eclipse of the known variable WHAT236 5760 is easy to miss in corrected QLP flux. The archive window measures 135 ppt in raw light and 1.84 ppt after correction. The deficit in the sector 24 full-frame image centers 0.59 arcseconds from the star. Sector 78 captures ingress 48 orbital cycles later. Aligning this partial event with the complete sector 24 profile gives about 30.80065 days. ASAS-SN measurements selected at that phase give median core losses of 0.191 mag across 20 cycles in green and 0.155 mag across seven cycles in V. VSX lists an eclipsing classification and a 30.76443-day period. The TESS period assumes the same ingress profile in both sectors. [TESS and ASAS-SN observations](298262982-eclipse.png) · [Measurements](298262982-ephemeris.csv) · [Sector image](../followup/298262982.png)

**TIC 154481910 and TIC 366896062.** Both large raw dips occur beside gaps and are strongly suppressed by correction. The three other covered Gaia-period predictions for TIC 154481910 lack a comparable dip. The sector 81 sky background for TIC 366896062 changes rapidly as the raw flux falls, and the original image sequence lacks both baseline intervals needed to establish a celestial source.
