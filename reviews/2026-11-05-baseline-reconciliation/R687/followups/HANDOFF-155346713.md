Sector 7 has two striking features. Its ingress, flat bottom and egress are visible across about 0.23 d. Near the sector end, a separate deeper ingress begins around BTJD 1515.75 and falls by roughly 10% before QLP stops at BTJD 1516.03583. An A-like center extrapolated from the sector-88 eclipse is near BTJD 1516.15, but the extrapolation is uncertain and the event center and egress are **not observed**. Sector 8 starts at BTJD 1517.93166, after the missing interval; it supplies no completion of that eclipse.

Identity-verified TESS-SPOC and Gaia-ID-targeted TGLC sector-7 light curves reproduce both features from differently processed versions of the same TESS photons. The complete dip is approximately 4.5–6.4% across their SAP, PDC and TGLC flux products; aperture-dependent depths are expected. A TESScut Q0 per-pixel template fit to 38 sector-7 cadences puts the complete-dip deficit centroid **5.2 arcsec** from the exact Gaia target coordinate and visibly on its PSF. The only G<14.7 neighbor in the 60-arcsec Gaia cone is G14.65 at 47.5 arcsec and displaced from the deficit. This is encouraging target attribution, with no calibrated localization uncertainty. The target has a G16.27 neighbor at 2.4 arcsec; its approximate G-band flux is too faint to explain a 5.7% blended loss by itself, but source attribution still requires a careful full review.

The expected B-like recurrence in sector 61 is **not a clean null**. Extrapolating the sector-7 center by 44 Gaia periods gives BTJD 2982.37282; QLP quality-zero data have a **0.21991-day gap, 2982.19259–2982.41249**, that covers the predicted core. Whether an egress should be visible after the gap depends on the true period and event timing. The sector-7 A-like ingress and sector-88 complete A-like eclipse suggest a compatible 65-cycle baseline, but the earlier center is unmeasured. No secure same-kind period fit or orbital match is claimed from these observations alone.

**Disposition:** high-priority retained eclipse-like candidate, pending full physical, alias, pixel and catalogue review. It is **not an accepted addition**. Keep the complete sector-7 B-like event, truncated A-like ingress, sector-88 eclipse, and sector-61 cadence gap together; the B-like event alone must not be treated as a proven recurring eclipse.
