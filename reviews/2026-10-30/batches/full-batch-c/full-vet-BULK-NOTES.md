# Bulk photometry and orbit analysis notes

QLP manifest: qlp-manifest.json; all 49 products downloaded and SHA-256 hashed. All resolved CSV periods match search P within rounding. Primary fit results are in each TIC/fit-results.json (raw SAP, individual events, ephemeris, blind census). Orbit-gates.json contains Gaia covariance comparisons, individual other-conjunction windows and fixed-template phase controls. Gaia SB1 uses six parameters, bit_index 127, 15 correlations; 339787086 uses its SB2 solution, bit_index 255, seven parameters and 21 correlations (period, gamma, K1, K2, e, omega, Tp). The unrelated Orbital solution is not substituted. Covariance ordering is from Gaia DR3 nss_two_body_orbit documentation: https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Gaia_archive/chap_datamodel/sec_dm_non--single_stars_tables/ssec_dm_nss_two_body_orbit.html.

Secondary-fit.json is an optimized, conditional phase/shape scan; report controls and event repetition, not only formal S/N. Primary-shape fixed-phase limits in orbit-gates.json are conditional, with off-phase scatter; coverage status is recorded per window. Geometry.json gives a coarse feasibility profile with fixed TIC stellar dimensions and free eclipse flux scales, not a posterior or direct mass/radius measurement. Individual timing and depth errors do not include all baseline/model uncertainty. Notably 339787086 primary sector-correlated residuals give chi2 1796.6/20; its period and conjunction discrepancy are real measurements but model-dependent. 97509220 and 117919258 periods come from only one sector each; extrapolation to Gaia epoch is poorly constrained.

Independent-manifest-results.json records exact MAST URI, URL, hashes, FITS target/sector and measurements at fitted QLP mid-times. Nine use SPOC or TESS-SPOC; 97509220 uses TARS s92. These are independent reductions of TESS data, not an independent observatory. No tests in this folder establish spatial attribution; the lead owns those gates. Scripts and logs provide details; FITS remain in ignored data/.
