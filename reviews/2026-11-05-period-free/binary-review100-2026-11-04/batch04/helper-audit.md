# Independent helper audit

H032 / TIC 372989764: quality-zero SAP local trapezoid centers and their linear cycle clock are in helper-local-fits.json and helper-clock.json. Four deep events span S39, 66, 103 and 104; adjacent S103/104 establish approximately one 28.2-day cycle and oppose nearby long-baseline integer aliases. The two visibly different eclipse depths prevent treating the shallow conjunction as another same-kind event. Gaia B prediction at the S39 deep event is 0.046 d later than the measured center, consistent within model limitations. These exploratory fits do not carry a calibrated period error.

An independent 5-by-5-pixel reduction of the S66 TESSCut gives deep ~29.5 ppt and shallow ~8.2 ppt, using the same photons as QLP, not new observing occasions. Difference centroids are 0.108 and 0.264 pixels from target, respectively, calculated by clipping positive differences and susceptible to bias. Gaia DR3 5923328421736889728 is 6.36 arcsec away (0.31 pixel), G=14.810 versus target 11.450, and could reproduce the deep signal with ~68% eclipse under approximate equal-response G-band flux ratios. Other capable stars are farther away. Centroid proximity alone does not resolve this close neighbor; attribution remains uncertain. Pixel WCS, source list, caveats and receipts are in helper-source-attribution.json, helper-shallow-attribution.json and helper-pixels/372989764/. No claim of precise deblending is warranted.

H031 and H040: previews and full-sector plots independently show their shared 3874.77 feature coinciding with many other targets and a broad sector systematic. Other flagged events in H031 fall at gaps/steps; H040 S101 is a discontinuity with instrumental recovery, S103 is broad structure and S13 sparse. The displayed raw contexts do not reveal a convincing recurring eclipse; common-mode counts alone are not a veto and do not exclude a binary.


H039 / TIC 454228598: S12 alternative 5x5 cutout extraction repeats the roughly 15 ppt asymmetric depression but shares photons with SAP. Quality-zero sampling starts at 1624.973, less than a day before its minimum, while the baseline already slopes downward; the irregular multiday recovery leaves startup/systematic versus astrophysical origin unresolved. Gaia 5873154098374360192 at 5.96 arcsec, G=13.718, has approximately 5.3% of target G flux and is capable of producing this depression. No reliable deblend or independent occurrence was established. See helper-h039-neighbors.json and helper-pixels/454228598/helper-aperture.png.
