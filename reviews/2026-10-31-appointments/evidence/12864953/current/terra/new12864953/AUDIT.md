# Independent review: TIC 12864953 sector 107

The newly observed B eclipse repeats the sector 69 event. In my assessment, confirmed is now supported by the bounded criteria: repeated matching on-target eclipses, photometric period compatible with Gaia, and physically plausible geometry. Existing 3.4σ phase-separation tension remains an unresolved orbital-model caveat rather than automatic rejection. The 3.4σ value was computed conditional on Gaia P in the earlier analysis; recomputing its significance with the new photometric period and uncertainties would be preferable, and do not claim it has vanished.

Inspected events.png and pixels.png, analyzed source and derived outputs, and independently checked FITS data using check.py. At B center 4271.61686, 52 central cadences (within 0.06 d) all have quality 0. Local background estimator median 133.569 inside vs 133.562 outside, outside scatter 0.566, no apparent central background excursion. Pixel deficit peaks at target; regression gives descriptive centroid offset 3.0 arcsec and target pixel 12.54 ppt. The centroid is not a calibrated exclusion radius; earlier Gaia neighbour/dilution analysis supplies complementary support. Fits yield 10.58–12.90 ppt across apertures, broadly compatible with s69 B around 10.8 ppt; the extractions share photons and are not independent observations. B shape and duration resemble s69. The 34-cycle difference (4271.6168606 - 3196.0997)/34 = 31.6328577 d, differing from Gaia P 31.624523 ± 0.005892 by about 1.4σ. Adjacent cycle counts imply 32.59143 and 30.72906 d, inconsistent with Gaia. The two-epoch period has no third-B O–C check; ±0.00015 d is a conservative working estimate, not a fully calibrated statistical interval.
