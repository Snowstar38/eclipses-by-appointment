# Batch 08 lead audit

The ten rows in `queue.csv` were reviewed without substitution. Eight are **CONFIRMED** and two **PROBABLE**; all ten bounded reviews are **COMPLETE**. The two probable outcomes (TIC 202634485 and TIC 403410574) have repeated real eclipses, measured periods, a same-photon alternative extraction and plausible Gaia geometry, but source attribution remains unresolved because capable neighbors lie within 10.1 and 12.2 arcsec, respectively. The physical verdict is separate from the central seven-group catalogue check; all ten have complete scoped checks and no identified prior eclipsing-binary match in `../shared-catalogues/coverage-matrix.csv`.

I checked each target's original QLP FITS identity and SHA256 against `download-receipts.json`. Targeted sectors 39 for TICs 30770290, 306980055 and 403410574 and sector 65 for TIC 457272901 were additionally downloaded, validated and recorded in `targeted-download-receipts.json`. Free-center trapezoid fits to representative quality-zero raw SAP flux generated `measurements.json`, `raw-windows.csv` and `raw-events.png` per target. The timing fit uses actual midpoints and historical integer-cycle labels. Its reported errors are conservative inputs from max(formal center covariance, half cadence), with covariance inflation for excess scatter; they are not calibrated total uncertainty. The named reference eclipse is not assumed to be the deeper primary.

The most material timing checks were TIC 30770290 and TIC 34759144. Sector-66 cycle 176 of TIC 30770290 has a real partial raw eclipse between two fitted events, supporting 8.3804 d against a simple doubled-cycle alias; it was not used as a precise midpoint. For TIC 34759144, the three measured centers imply 57 then 37 cycles at 19.33997 d; nearby integer-cycle alternatives have gap-period disagreements at least 0.0248 d versus 0.000030 d for the selected assignment. `alias-audit.json`, `34759144-alias-enumeration.json` and the independent helper check retain the numeric basis. No exhaustive all-alias search or all-sector fit is claimed.

Historical TESScut difference images and original cutouts were checked for all ten. Each report lists every capable Gaia neighbor in the 60-arcsec screen, its approximate intrinsic eclipse requirement and its distance from the measured deficit centroid. These are descriptive localization tests, not calibrated centroid errors. The alternative 1x1, 3x3 and 5x5 pixel reductions use the same TESS photons; TIC 202634485 in particular has strong aperture and baseline sensitivity, so unequal depths are not treated as a separate astrophysical detection. Full-source-specific Gaia NSS covariance underlies `gaia-geometry.json`; conjunction timing and approximate duration/mass-function checks support plausibility without an arbitrary sigma veto.

The independent Terra helper reviewed raw plots, timing and alias cases, difference panels, aperture depths, geometry and final report/result numbers. The specific causal wording on TIC 202634485 was softened after that audit. `helper-audit.md` records its findings and optional contributor note. `source-hashes.json` and `SHA256SUMS.json` bind the historical sources, original cutouts, downloaded QLP files, code and delivered artifacts. Root retains explicit acceptance and integration authority.
