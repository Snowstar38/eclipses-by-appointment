## Deciding case

Z019 has two same-kind shallow eclipses, not three: the intervening deeper event is the opposite conjunction. The shallow interval is 24.291620859 d, versus Gaia 24.285909256 ± 0.007583387 d. The formal interval error after a 0.002 d per-event floor is 0.002828427 d; a separate 0.02 d shape/baseline allowance is heuristic, not calibrated confidence. No long-baseline timing scatter or singular-fit precision is inferred. The three conjunction offsets are 0.478, 0.173 and 0.483 d, versus source-specific local covariance sigmas 0.975, 0.898 and 0.983 d. Gaia mass function 0.19956 solar masses and distinct eclipse depths are compatible with a stellar binary; no detailed stellar model is claimed.

The closest concrete capable Gaia neighbor is G=12.523 at 25.07 arcsec, about 1.21 pixels away. Its idealized required intrinsic eclipse is about 0.143, so brightness alone cannot dismiss it. Three background-corrected deficit images stay 0.085–0.234 pixel from the target and 1.25–1.36 pixels from that neighbor under four window choices. Their direction, peak and stability distinguish the target from this resolved capable source. This supports confirmation under the bounded paper protocol. The empirical centroid spread is not a calibrated PRF confidence interval, and unresolved companions remain a general limitation. Different reductions of these photons are not independent observations. Both shallow events are complete; the deeper event's egress is interrupted by a gap.
