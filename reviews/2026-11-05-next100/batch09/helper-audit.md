## Follow-up: pixel attribution, morphology and control stars

Four independent TESSCut downloads succeeded; the Gaia DR3 100.8-arcsec, G < 18 neighborhood queries succeeded at the Heidelberg mirror (ESA TAP returned HTTP 503). Source coordinates and difference images, core/side counts and exact hashes are recorded in `helper-pixel.json`, with code `helper-pixel.py` and `helper-gaia.py`. Positive local sky-subtracted target aperture flux is 326,976, 28,437, 28,285 and 128,288 counts for Q082 S11, Q083 S57, Q083 S84 and Q090 S41 respectively; median sky is finite, and WCS places the target inside all cutouts. These are blended-aperture fluxes, not deblended target fluxes. Difference centroids are 0.179, 0.159, 0.081 and 0.398 pixels from the target, respectively; this supports association with the target's blended image but does not resolve close capable neighbors.

Q082 has a G12.668 neighbor 0.877 pixels away, approximate Gaia G flux ratio 0.00811. Q083 has a G15.236 neighbor 0.885 pixels away, ratio 0.00549. Both ratios are of order or above the observed few-ppt variation, so a sufficiently deep neighbor signal remains possible and fractional-pixel centroids do not establish ownership. Q090 has G12.669 at 0.075 pixel and G11.190 at 0.852 pixel, ratios 0.0132 and 0.0515. G ratios are approximate and bandpass dependent; flux loss and brightening require different bounds. The Q090 broad difference image itself cannot isolate cause. These same-photon images add spatial evidence, not new observing occasions.

On closer inspection, the S57 2872.425 and 2877.295 troughs are locally roughly symmetric and eclipse-like; their rounded bottoms and asymmetric changing baseline, differing widths, and changing ~2.2–2.6-day spacing across the train support oscillation as a competing explanation, but do not prove no eclipse. Neither the smoothed minima nor Gaia period establishes a secure eclipse clock. The available evidence cannot uniquely attribute the variation or resolve the competing interpretations.

Q090 S41 controls materially strengthen the common systematic interpretation. Of 160 cached S41 comparison targets examined, TIC 122682175 (camera 1 CCD 2) and TIC 164669150 (camera 2 CCD 3) have correlations 0.973 and 0.969 with Q090 on 0.25-day binned raw SAP. Their rises and falls line up through both halves of the sector, including the large reset near 2433. These are selected high-correlation examples, not an unbiased prevalence estimate, and do not rule out additional intrinsic variations. See `helper-q090-controls.png` and `helper-q090-controls.json` for image, selection, camera/CCD and source hashes. This follow-up supersedes the earlier sentence that no pixel extraction was performed; Gaia conjunction geometry remains untested.
