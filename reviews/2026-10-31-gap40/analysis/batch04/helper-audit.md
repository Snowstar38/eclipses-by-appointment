# Independent helper audit — batch 04

This is a bounded audit of the initial widened-window measurements, parity and priority pixel cutouts; it is not an independent reduction of TESS photons. `helper/audit.py` reproduces `helper/audit-numbers.json` from the ten measurement files and the two sector-98 cutouts. That JSON records SHA-256 of each timing input, both pixel inputs and historical difference-image JSON; subsequent lead revisions may change those inputs. Historical cycles are those of the search ephemeris, not evidence of independent orbital-cycle identification.

The measurement script filters quality == 0, fits individual local trapezoids, and estimates event errors as the larger of formal covariance and half a cadence. Timing is then a weighted straight-line fit, with covariance inflated when reduced chi-square exceeds one. This is a descriptive error model, not a calibrated coverage interval. Fits near boundaries, sparse egress, singular covariance and time-correlated local noise can defeat the apparent precision. Sector-specific dilution and trends affect depths; fitting parity medians do not prove equal eclipses or the orbital alias. All Gaia SB1 periods in `gaia-geometry.json` match the photometric trial periods much more closely than twice those periods; agreement is a useful constraint rather than proof of source identity.

The 30.54 arcsec neighbor is closer and requires separate consideration, as in the saved difference-image map. Gaia period 13.74849 ± 0.00396 d is compatible in scale with the photometric value; conjunction uncertainties must retain their stated model assumptions.

The widened cycle 93 timing covariance is singular (217,601 d) and is not a useful timing constraint.

- TIC 300969635: global even/odd medians 214.7/232.2 ppt are confounded by sector coverage and dilution; sector 37 cycles 133/134/135 are 246.4/249.7/248.2 ppt. Other batch parity medians and exact cycles are in the JSON; limited samples do not rule out secondaries.

The cutout calculation uses absolute time distance <0.3 duration for in-eclipse and 1–2.5 durations for out-of-eclipse, per-pixel median difference and quality-zero cadences; local centroid uses only positive differences within two pixels of the rounded target. These simple diagnostics are sensitive to common-mode backgrounds and the chosen window and do not provide centroid errors.
