# Frozen selection and priority provenance

These tables preserve the full score and identity context for the 250 reviewed targets, the near misses, and the unreviewed candidates. The 2,984-row [inclusive parent](candidate-backlog.csv) is a **mixed screening backlog**, not 2,984 period-free eclipse detections. It contains 1,000 multi-sector dip flags, 1,141 single-sector dip flags, 712 brightening leads, 58 period-fold-only flags, and 73 near-threshold period-fold leads. A multi-sector flag means flagged windows in at least two sectors; it does not establish repeated eclipses of the same kind. [All 6,186 screened targets](all-targets.csv) also retain 3,202 nonflags and file failures. This is the available cached population after historical ledger/review exclusions, not a complete sky survey or an unbiased yield sample.

The period-free detector used quality-zero, positive finite `SAP_FLUX`; ten-minute bins where needed; trial durations 0.06, 0.12, 0.24, 0.48, 0.96, and 1.6 days; a core of ±0.5 duration and sidebands at 0.75–1.75 durations on each side. A supported flag required at least three core points, four points in each sideband, at least half-duration sampled support, no internal gap above `max(3 cadences, 0.5 duration)` in core or sides, signed depth at least 0.001, and signed contrast at least five. Contrast used the larger of sideband residual RMS and robust adjacent-difference noise; it is a selection score, not significance. Up to four dip and two brightening windows were retained per sector after overlap suppression. The original [detector](bulk_events.py), [backlog builder](build_backlog.py), [frozen configuration](population-scope.json), [harvest summary](BACKLOG-SUMMARY.json), and [common-mode indicators](target-features.csv) specify the implementation. Common-mode coincidence means a same-sign flagged window in another TIC within 0.25 days in the same sector; detector separation is retained, with no automatic veto.

Period-fold flags applied only where that older selected 24–60 day cached method ran, not to every one of the 6,186 targets. Its flag was `z ≥ 5` or `(depth > 5000 ppm and SNR > 3 × positive null median)`; a nonflag with maximum P/P2 `z ≥ 4` entered the near-threshold lane. The [fold code](fold24_60.py) and [raw-window rank configuration](fold-rank-config.json) retain the other settings and tier definitions. A missing fold attempt is unknown, not a failed fold test. No period, magnitude, coverage or noise cut was added to the eligible period-free cached population.

The [parent dispositions](all-parent-dispositions.csv) identify 80 historical identity exclusions, 100 completed initial reviews, and 2,804 eligible targets left for scheduling. The initial 100 deliberately sampled 30 cross-method dip, 20 other multi-sector dip, 20 single-sector dip, ten fold-only, ten near-fold, and ten brightening leads. Its outcomes informed the later ordering, so that sample does not independently validate the priority rule. The 80 exclusions stay visible with their cached-primary-match and prior-note flags; a catalogue nonmatch here is not novelty clearance.

The [complete priority table](ranked-remaining.csv) retains every one of the 2,804 remaining targets, including scores, source identities, original group, alternate sector-first rank, and reason. Cross-method dips come first, then other multi-sector dips, then single-sector, fold-only, near-fold, and brightening lanes. Within a dip lane the order is descending best dip contrast; fold lanes use fold `z`; brightening uses brightening contrast. Numeric TIC breaks ties. There are **no new score thresholds or vetoes** in this ordering. The [first 100](priority-first100.csv) comprise 72 cross-method and 28 other multi-sector dips; [ranks 1–150](priority-first150.csv) comprise the same 72 plus 78 other multi-sector dips and match the three completed priority review queues exactly. The [20 exploration targets](exploration20.csv) were separately hash-selected, five from each later lane, and remain within the 2,804.

Run `python verify_selection.py` here to reconstruct the 2,984 → 80 + 100 + 2,804 split, ranking, first 150, alternate ordering and exploration set from the exported tables. The [export manifest](selection-manifest.csv) pairs every sealed original file hash with its public export hash. Path fields in the exported score tables use `source-only:bulk-results/<TIC>.json` for omitted per-target detector outputs. Raw FITS, those detector outputs, and private working paths are not in this compact export, so the detector cannot be rerun offline from this archive alone. The score and scheduling reconstruction is complete from the published CSVs.
