# Independent helper audit: U001–U009

Scope: independently inspected all nine lead raw-context images and extracted representative TESSCut difference images. `helper_cuts.py`, `helper-cuts-receipts.json`, `helper_pixels.py`, and `helper-pixels-results.json` give requests, SHA-256, actual cadence masks, WCS positions, difference matrices and plots. In/out medians are on the same photons as QLP and are an alternative reduction, not independent observing occasions. Image maxima are pixel-scale localization, not a precision centroid fit or an exhaustive close-blend exclusion. Lead owns verdicts, timing, coverage, geometry and catalogue status.

- **U001 / 259614821:** S38 deep event is resolved in raw SAP, with multiple similar deep occasions in S39/65/100/101/102. S38 difference peak (3,4) coincides with target WCS (3.04,3.71), while another bright source lies toward the cutout edge. Supports target attribution at pixel scale; unresolved close companions remain possible. S38 event and background are independently extracted from TESSCut. Do not use agreement with Gaia period alone to claim geometry.
- **U002 / 371856921:** S23 clear flat-bottomed event; difference peak (3,3) matches target (3.00,3.31), supported by only three in-event 30-minute cadences. Repeat remains unestablished; event and alternative reduction share photons. Single-event aliases and actual available coverage must remain explicit.
- **U003 / 412521418:** S7/34/61/87/88 raw selected minima occur amid a broad ~1.79 d wave. S34 difference peak (5,4) is near known EA ASASSN-V J072304.38-221627.8 at (4.55,4.48), displaced from target (3.52,3.42); neighbour has 1.7916 d catalogue period at 30.57 arcsec. This is positive evidence for contamination by a specific eclipsing neighbor, not evidence of a 35.7 d Gaia eclipse. Follow-up could check any separate target signal.
- **U004 / 86182628:** S22/S49 clear similar deep dips; S49 difference peak (3,3) near target (3.39,3.48). Two same-kind events alone leave integer cycle aliases unless other actual coverage discriminates. Exact prior PRSA match makes this a known recovery regardless of physical classification. Pixel data do not by themselves rule out close blends.
- **U005 / 26486304:** S14–83 repeat two distinct deep eclipse kinds; both S81 difference patterns (BTJD ~3514.46 and 3527.585) peak at (4,4) near the target (3.45,3.90) and look nearly identical spatially, supporting a common source. Precise relative loss/source fits were not done. Lead correctly notes opposite-kind separation evolves between S14/15 and S75+, so do not treat opposite kinds as a single constant half-period clock, or blindly pool them; possible apsidal or source explanations remain open.
- **U006 / 356801225:** Repeated deep sharp events in raw S40/54/80/81; S80 is cut at the beginning. S81 difference peak (3,4) at target (3.16,3.79), away from EA neighbor (5.54,2.74) at 52.25 arcsec and P 0.6754534 d. This supports target association at TESS resolution. S54 corrected flux is visibly distorted; raw and independent TESSCut event should be distinguished, and aperture pixels cannot exclude unresolved close blends.
- **U007 / 331643517:** Deep and flanking shallower dips recur with ~1.1145 d clock, not the Gaia 53.25 d orbital period. In S56 difference peaks (6,2), substantially displaced from target WCS (3.707,3.473) and consistent with nearby TIC 331643515 at (5.474,2.461), 41.995 arcsec away. Shared table4 lists this neighbor at P-TESS 1.114507 d; it is the short eclipse train's natural source. The independently recorded exact-target IJspeert prior should not be conflated with this neighbor's TESS signal. The result does not rule out a separate eclipsing target signal.
- **U008 / 343187602:** S24 shows one apparent small compact dip plus post-event overshoot; repeat unestablished. The S24 TESSCut difference image is dominated by a spatially varying common sky change (~26–38 e/s), so this simple uncorrected difference is not a reliable source localization or a sensitive negative test. S76 cutout has near-zero target flux per lead and is not a null. Further valid background modeling and another observation are needed.
- **U009 / 768070:** S106 and S108 new independent observing occasions show strong narrow dips; difference peaks trace target in both rotations, with positive difference centroids ~0.16–0.18 pixel from target. Independent fit estimates should use lead timing, since minima can fall anywhere in a flat/noisy core. S98 low interval also shows a target-centered excess deficit on top of ~20 e/s common offset, but quality-zero data gap 4038.62739–4038.85655 interrupts the event before complete egress and the post-gap level changes. S98 is a compatible partial occasion, not an independently precise full eclipse. Tentatively PROBABLE if lead confirms recurrent morphology: unresolved orbital geometry, alias and close-blend questions prevent confirmation. See `768070/helper_pixels.py`, `helper-pixel-768070.json`, `helper_s98.py`, `helper-s98.json` and plotted differences.
