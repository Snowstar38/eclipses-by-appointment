# Independent helper audit

I read the campaign brief, delegation, privacy rule and current methods sections 2.7–2.8. I independently inspected the representative raw and difference-image contact sheets, measurement records, cycle assignments, source and alternative-reduction descriptions, neighbor geometry and Gaia geometry. This is an audit of existing extractions, not an independent observation or a second extraction of the photons.

`helper-audit/check.py` independently reconstructs the weighted timing lines using centered sums, checks the ten result rows against measurements, verifies that used midpoints lie within the saved raw windows, and rehashes the QLP source files. Its output `helper-audit/numeric-check.json` records counts, sectors, fitted periods, epochs and errors. All ten reproduce the reported values. Formal covariance/half-cadence inputs and chi-square inflation are fitting allowances, not total uncertainties. One singular center fit for TIC 375058760 is excluded. TIC 48870240 has measured cycles 0, 1, 5–8, including four consecutive events; TIC 178199444 has cycles 108, 110 and 111. Neither is a single-cycle case.

TIC 16809274: even historical cycles 0, 2, 248, 250 and 252 have fitted depths 57.73, 56.15, 61.88, 61.83 and 58.35 ppt. Odd cycles 249, 251 and 253 are deeper at 86.66, 80.93 and 80.49 ppt. Thus the selected same-kind line has 14.725871972 d and BTJD 1714.5124553 refers to the shallower eclipse; the older 7.362473 d search period is the alternating-event interval, not the orbital period. The numeric schema field `primary_epoch_btjd` is a legacy label and the eclipse-kind caveat must survive export. The cutout stack mixes kinds. Gaia period 14.728737 ± 0.002985 d and conjunction offset -0.254 d versus propagated 0.301 d are compatible within the stated approximate model.

The ten difference images show deficits concentrated near target positions. Target-versus-capable-neighbor separations and listed aperture tests support attribution at TESS resolution, with TIC 233462817's noisy 0.53-pixel offset explicitly limiting localization. An intrinsic neighbor eclipse fraction cannot be compared directly to a blended pixel fractional loss. TESScut is an alternative reduction of the same TESS photons. Gaia geometry remains approximate and covariance-dependent, not a sigma veto; TIC 233462817 and TIC 375058760 have notable offsets retained in their geometry records. Flagged or uncovered windows are not clean non-detections. No exhaustive all-sector or all-alias analysis is claimed.
