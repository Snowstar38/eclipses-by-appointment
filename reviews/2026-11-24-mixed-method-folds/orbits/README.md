# Gaia orbital timing

The TAP query returned one single-lined spectroscopic solution for each of the ten supplied Gaia DR3 source IDs. Each has a six-parameter covariance (bit index 127). We used the published period, eccentricity, argument of periastron and time of periastron with their joint covariance. We converted the TESS fold epoch from TDB to TCB and measured time from Gaia's J2016.0 reference epoch. [Archive query and hash](query.json) · [Gaia table documentation](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Gaia_archive/chap_datamodel/sec_dm_non--single_stars_tables/ssec_dm_nss_two_body_orbit.html)

For an edge-on orbit the two conjunctions have true longitude 90° and 270°. We solve Kepler's equation in the true-to-mean-anomaly direction for each, take the nearest predicted cycle, and propagate the published covariance through 20,000 Gaussian draws per source, retaining physical eccentricities. The central 95% range describes the model at the archived TESS epoch. Inclination, orbit systematics, additional components and uncertainty in the TESS trial ephemeris are outside the propagation. Three stars have at least one interval overlapping the trial eclipse core, including two with multi-day timing uncertainty. These overlaps do not demonstrate an eclipse.
