# Ground checks for the six open leads in batches 06–10

None of the six is confirmed by a fixed-phase dip in the available ASAS-SN or ZTF photometry. All estimated core-versus-wing 95% intervals overlap zero. Available ASAS-SN g-band scatter is 31–95 mmag per night, with typical confidence limits much wider than the shallow TESS losses. These are tests of particular extrapolated trial clocks. They leave room for a low-amplitude, evolving, shifted, or unresolved-source signal.

The batch folders provide the period, center and core width for every clock, including the local-fit, pilot and fold-screen variants. The Gaia-period tests assume the catalog period and fix phase from a TESS window selected before the ground analysis. They do not predict a conjunction from the orbit. None of the clocks was adjusted using the ground data. Candidate epoch and period uncertainty can broaden extrapolated phase, especially for two-event aliases. The intervals here are conditional on the recorded clock and do not include source dilution or that ephemeris uncertainty.

Run `python -B sky/mixed-150-2026-11-24/batch06/ground-open/ground_open.py tmp/mixed150-ground` from the workspace root. The script requires NumPy and Astropy. Add `--fallback-cache` with an auxiliary cache directory if needed. All 12 catalog-query outcomes and uncompressed hashes are in [cache-receipts.csv](cache-receipts.csv). For provenance, the upstream queries are in [ASAS-SN Sky Patrol retrieval](../../ground/asassn.py) and [ZTF retrieval](../../ground/fetch.py). The cones are 4 and 2.16 arcsec respectively, centered on the nominated position.

Keep finite ASAS-SN quality G and ZTF catflags=0 measurements with positive magnitude errors below 0.1 mag. Split by object and filter. Convert ZTF MJD and ASAS-SN JD to approximate geocentric BJD_TDB using the nominated coordinates and UTC assumption. Midpoint and heliocentric timing convention uncertainty is only minutes, small relative to these 0.48–1.60 d ground windows. Group by detector and observing year, subtract the median, then take one residual per night. Compute the median core minus the median of nearby wings, with positive contrast meaning dimmer. Resample complete orbital-cycle blocks for the nominal 95% intervals. The bootstrap keeps same-cycle points grouped and the result is a targeted contrast estimate. No trials correction is included. See the [contrasts and coverage](phase-contrasts.csv) and [sampled cycles](per-cycle.csv).
