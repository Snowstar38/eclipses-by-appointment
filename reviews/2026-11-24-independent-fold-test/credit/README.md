All 11 VizieR catalogue fields passed their positive controls in cone searches of radius 15 arcminutes. Four complete local lists (IJspeert 2021 and 2024, Prša 2022, and the project catalogue) contained no row within that radius for any of the three positions. The live Villanova TIC searches returned no exact entry; ExoFOP returned no TOI, CTOI, follow-up product or note for these TICs. ADS full-text queries of the resolved target aliases yielded no matches. SIMBAD independently links the first target to Gaia's multiplicity paper. The checks cover the specified identities, positions and catalogues. Aperture-event recovery alone cannot establish discovery priority or the eclipsing host.
