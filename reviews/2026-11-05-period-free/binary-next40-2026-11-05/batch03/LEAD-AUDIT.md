# Batch03 lead audit

Exact sealed queue F021-F030, all ten reviewed. Raw SAP/quality/time coverage, local event fits, source-specific Gaia conjunctions, alternative QLP reductions, catalogue evidence and selected TESSCut difference images were checked. Detector flags were interpreted only after the raw context and common-event comparison.

F021/F024/F026/F029 repeatedly share the sector-94 BTJD 3875.8 gap morphology; F021/F026/F029 also share the S67 BTJD 3146.8 dip. F025 has two gap-boundary events. These are refutations of the flagged eclipse hypotheses, not claims that their stars cannot be binaries. F022 is a known VSX ESD recovery with convincing deep repeated eclipses but a source-specific Gaia phase tension and QLP correction suppression. F023 is a known TOI-5149 EB recovery with eight raw same-kind timings, alternate QLP support and target-centered difference flux. Its eight covered half-clock and thirteen covered third-clock extra-phase windows are flat to within 0.27 ppt maximum versus about 10.7 ppt real dips; S16 observed and null windows were visually checked in lead-null-windows.png. F027 is a real broad variable with insecure eclipse interpretation. F028 has many real narrow dips and a uniquely favored 2.065942-d photometric cadence, but its Gaia 35.0856-d orbit and pixel ownership remain unresolved. F030 dips are the known 0.540070-d VSX EB 13.85 arcsec away, supported by actual WCS difference flux.

All periods and depths in RESULTS.csv are photometric measurements or bounded diagnostics, never copied Gaia periods. Blank errors reflect model/systematic or alias limitations. Same photons in QLP and TESSCut are alternative reductions, not independent observing occasions. All ten bounded reviews are complete; probable/plausible verdicts describe unresolved science. Source files and receipts are hashed. The batch does not edit the shared catalogue or old cohorts.
