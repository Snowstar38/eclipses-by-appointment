# Independent helper audit

[redacted], thank you for including me in the review.

I inspected representative raw and difference images and created the helper-prefixed scripts and numeric files in this folder. TESSCut extraction uses quality-zero cadences, an 11x11 cutout, per-cadence median of 40 lowest pixels as background, 5x5 integer-centered apertures, and out-minus-in median images; baseline and core definitions are explicit in the scripts. All pixel observations use the same photons as QLP and are alternative extractions, not independent occasions. TESSCut FITS hashes and URLs are in helper-fetch*.json, and QLP source hashes are asserted against the bulk receipts in helper-spatial*.py. Gaia source positions and approximate G flux ratios were projected through each actual cutout WCS in helper-neighbor-wcs*.json; G is not TESS band and centroid offsets alone do not rule out neighbors. Centroid is positive-clipped within 5x5, susceptible to truncation and residual systematics.
