# Governing review brief

Review 70 disjoint candidate targets in queue.csv, in seven batches of ten. Queue positions are not verdicts. Preserve raw receipts, adapted scripts, checksums and target-specific reports. Do not change source tables, the paper, or previous outputs. Public outputs must use anonymous coordinator wording under [local working path omitted; see provenance receipts]

The current paper Conventions govern physical assessment: remeasure repeated events from raw flux; derive the period from eclipses; test difference imaging against neighbouring sources capable of producing the eclipse; obtain at least one independent pipeline; and assess geometry against the Gaia orbit. A handful of representative events and sufficient source tests can decide the evidence. Existing valid receipts may be reused after checking target and assumptions. No exhaustive every-event/every-sector fitting, compulsory new TESScut extraction, covariance Monte Carlo, detailed physics modelling, ephemerides or exhaustive literature work is mandatory.

Use CONFIRMED, PLAUSIBLE or REFUTED from the evidence. Report incomplete execution separately from unresolved scientific ambiguity. Characterization limitations do not veto a strong EB; never force confirmations. State precisely what was observed and what was inferred.

Catalogue coverage is centrally coordinated for seven scopes: VSX; SIMBAD primary object type; Gaia DR3 eclipsing binaries; Prsa2022; IJspeert2024 J/A+A/691/A242/obaf-eb1; Kostov2025 vetted J/ApJS/279/50/table3 and table4; ASAS-SN II/366/catv2021. Kostov table2 is unvetted and not prior-EB evidence. SB* and ROT alone are not EB types. Missing or failed services are unknown rather than negative. Physical verdict and novelty status are separate.

Each batch delivers exactly ten target reports and ten result rows, provenance and hashes. Leads review helper measurements and deciding evidence. No publication, commits or source-paper edits are authorized here.
