# TIC 630314437 S108: original stamp and FFI check

The signed-SAP seed at BTJD 4312.1726524 is accompanied by a very bright positive moving source, mostly clipped by the upper edge of the 11x11 target stamp. Its motion is directly visible in `diagnostic.png`, and a wider independent extraction of the same TESS camera 1 CCD 3 calibrated full-frame images confirms the track in `wide-ffi.png`. Five full-frame fitted positions and spacecraft UTC timestamps are in `wide-positions.csv`; these are approximate Gaussian centroids using the TESScut static cutout WCS, not calibrated PRF fits or sub-arcsecond astrometry. FFI frames occur ~40 seconds after stamp-window median times. The [full-frame script](../../wide_track.py) retrieves the wider TESScut product. The original TPF hash and clipped integer-pixel positions are in evidence.json. The stamp edge peaks are substantially biased and should not replace the full-frame positions.

RAW_CNTS aperture medians 851089 to 851408.5, change +319.5 counts. Median of sums need not equal sum of medians; these values use the exact requested windows, not the narrower frames in evidence.json. These measurements indicate the apparent negative background-subtracted flux is largely explained by the sky/background estimate, not a comparable loss of restored counts. They are observational evidence, not proof of every pipeline step.
