# Cloud72 completion: review evidence

The 72-target inventory retains 17 prior adjudications and indexes 55 newly accepted reviews. `FULL-RESULTS.csv` links each of the 55 reports; the original batch paths, numeric outputs, scripts, and catalogue receipts are retained. All six batches were accepted: 20 confirmed, seven probable, 11 plausible and 17 refuted. Two confirmed reviews are known catalogue recoveries and excluded from new headline additions.

The final paper sample comprises 372 systems (345 confirmed and 27 probable), all scoped uncatalogued; 13 plausible cases remain outside it. The seven catalogue scopes and their bounds are explained in `shared-catalogues/README.md`. The `prior-gap-recovery` directory documents the 13 earlier targets. The dated tier-consistency audit reconciles eight earlier probable cases while preserving historical reports.

Sol and Terra performed the measurements, Astra supervised the work, and Fable performed the original discovery and screening. The project coordinator did not perform the agent measurements.

Selected compact panels are included; raw FITS, cubes, arrays, archives and bulk plots are excluded. Source URLs and identifiers remain in the receipts. Scripts may require configuration and retrieval of omitted data. The manifest records separate source and exported SHA-256 hashes and identifies sanitization. Local workstation paths and private names have been removed.
