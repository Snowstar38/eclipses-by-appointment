# Batch 07

The QLP aperture of TIC 257808668 contains a 3.19798973 d signal from **TIC 257808672 (Gaia DR3 1786928913098116864)**, about 37 arcsec away. A Gaussian-plus-linear fit to eight archived SAP minima gives epoch 2802.5393100 BTJD, median FWHM 0.17026 d and |O−C| ≤0.0021 d. All 16 well-covered primary windows show a 6.02–7.45 ppt median SAP deficit and 3.54–7.48 ppt in corrected QLP. Eight of these windows were withheld from the timing fits. Thirteen well-covered midway windows give consistent shallower SAP deficits of 1.55–2.58 ppt and 0.75–1.79 ppt corrected.

**Pixel-level profiles.** Independent quality-screened fits use separate Gaussian templates at the astrometric positions, additional field sources and terms for plane background and image shifts. The primary losses are 17.1 and 17.9 e⁻/s from the neighbor in S55 and S82. Median same-phase depths at the nominated TIC stay within 0.18 e⁻/s of zero. The secondary losses are 6.6 and 6.4 e⁻/s from the neighbor and within 0.50 e⁻/s of zero for the target. Individual difference-frame centroid medians land 0.75 and 1.55 arcsec from the neighbor at the primary phase, and 1.78 and 2.09 arcsec from it at the secondary. The secondary has eight supported windows in S55 and six in S82 under the pixel coverage criterion.

An outside-dip two-harmonic fit follows the broad evolving waveform. Relative to that fit, the median primary residuals are −17.1 and −16.9 e⁻/s and secondary residuals −4.4 and −3.6 e⁻/s. The broad modulation changes between sectors while the narrow primary and shallower half-phase loss remain, supporting an eclipsing binary interpretation in addition to any rotational variability. These descriptive template amplitudes use detector flux units because the deblended quiescent flux scale is sensitive to scene assumptions.
