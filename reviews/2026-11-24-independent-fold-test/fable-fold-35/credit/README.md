# Prior-source checks

We matched 35 nominal apertures to Gaia sources and queried their Gaia DR3 variability and orbital records. Eleven VizieR tables also returned their known positive controls when queried in 15-arcminute cones. Full cone results include unrelated neighbors. An exact machine-candidate entry exists for 25 TICs in Kostov et al. Table 2. This includes all eight with ground recoveries. Its authors described that table as unvetted. [Cone results and queries](wide.json) · [Gaia and local comparisons](all-sources.json) · [Published table definitions](https://vizier.cfa.harvard.edu/viz-bin/VizieR-3?-source=J/ApJS/279/50)

The live Villanova lookup returned one exact target record, TIC 237115356. Its detail page marks signal 1 outside the EB catalogue. The existence of a target page alone does not establish an accepted eclipse period. For each of the 16 targets followed up in the pixels we also checked available target aliases through ADS, SIMBAD and ExoFOP. These exact-target checks supplement the positional searches. Published records under other names, unresolved companions, and additional surveys can still bear on credit.
