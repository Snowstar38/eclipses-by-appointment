# Batch 10

TIC 248646032 is a continuous ~0.94 d variable with a strong ~0.470 d corrected harmonic across three sectors. TIC 229506674 has deep short events in S76 that survive correction in attenuated form, with independent flat predictions at its supplied periods. A single-sector signal alone cannot establish a long orbital period.

TIC 331825104 and TIC 156003438 remain uncertain. The first has large but dissimilar SAP troughs. Gaia-like timing between some selected events is accompanied by gaps and a discordant S7 prediction. The second shows 27.3 d near-timing between selected broad dips, but its S96 feature is a shared disturbance and S2 contains a covered miss. Other candidates commonly track gap recovery and shared instrumental features. TIC 281709735 also displays a separate ~3.44 d SAP modulation.

The ten adjudications are in [outcomes.csv](outcomes.csv). Per-window observed and predicted measurements are in [event-evidence.csv](event-evidence.csv) and [predicted-windows.csv](predicted-windows.csv). [Shared windows](common-windows.csv) list comparisons with other reviewed targets in the same sector. The figures in `plots/` use blue for SAP and orange for QLP correction.
