# Independent helper audit: Q075 and Q077–Q079

TESSCut flux difference images use quality-zero FFI cadences, median outside minus median inside; pixel coordinates zero-based, WCS from APERTURE extension. `helper-difference.py` reproduces images and values; short-period folds use approximate clocks, so these images establish localization of the repeating loss, not precise eclipse ephemerides. Positive-flux centroids are descriptive, not fitted PRF positions, and do not alone exclude close neighbors. ZIP sources, Gaia cone output, scripts and numeric arrays are hashed in `helper-source-manifest.json`. Time coverage described below is cutout coverage, not a sensitive null of missing events.

* Q077, TIC 270523058: S14 at 0.641 d, 191 in and 622 out good FFIs. Target WCS (5.840,5.810), loss centroid (4.042,4.023), peak (4,4), a 2.54-pixel displacement. Centroid sky (293.368088,44.003643). Gaia DR3 2126300770519521024 G=12.122746 is 5.05 arcsec from this centroid; target Gaia 2126300907958489216 G=11.409027 is 52.33 arcsec away. Approximate Gaia G flux ratio neighbor/target 0.519. Its brightness and actual spatial loss implicate the neighbor as the repeating short-period source. Distinct from the target's 30.0295-d Gaia orbit. Finite difference morphology and approximate fold are not a precision PRF fit.
* Q078, TIC 295351347: S11, 0.692 d, 162/560 FFIs in/out. Target WCS (5.456,5.643), loss centroid (5.076,5.716), peak (6,6), offset 0.39 pixel. A source near the target is implicated but the centroid does not distinguish close capable neighbors. Gaia cone query and VizieR fallback timed out. This is not source ownership proof.
* Q079, TIC 280051953: S13 measured eclipse BTJD 1663.32049, 34 core and 121 comparison FFIs; target (5.075,5.065), difference centroid (5.130,5.097), peak (5,5), offset 0.064 pixel. Gaia neighbor query timed out, so close-source exclusion remains unresolved. Gaia SB1 source 4640830021527110272 has P=28.3418271 d, e=0.0126928, omega=188.63979 deg, t_periastron=-7.45587 d relative to J2016.0 = JD 2457389.0. Assuming i=90 deg, edge-on conjunctions have true longitude 90/270 deg; Kepler's equation gives nearest nominal conjunction 0.102 d before BTJD 1663.32049. Gaia t_periastron and omega have large correlated errors, and inclination is unknown: this nominal comparison is compatible but is not a full uncertainty calculation or precise orbital-geometry confirmation. Official Gaia documentation: https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Gaia_archive/chap_datamodel/sec_dm_non--single_stars_tables/ssec_dm_nss_two_body_orbit.html. Earlier BTJD 388.5 epoch communicated in error was corrected to BTJD 389.0.
* Q075, TIC 72497715: S99 differences for BTJD 4053.25 and 4068.06 have centroids (5.543,4.075) and (6.104,4.092), both peak (6,3), target WCS (5.594,5.132). Good-quality core/comparison counts 130/556 and 129/511. Similar displaced morphology raises a nearby-source concern, but PRF asymmetry, structured baseline, and unknown neighbors prevent decisive ownership. Both events use the same sector, so they do not establish independent-sector recurrence. No claim from fractional-pixel ratios.

Lead reports independent alternate reductions: Q077/Q078 retain eclipse morphology in DET_FLUX and SYS_RM_FLUX; Q079 S67 DET_FLUX suppresses but SYS_RM_FLUX retains the event. Suppression alone is not artifact proof. No source-specific Gaia neighbor data acquired for Q075, Q078 or Q079. No independent study of Q074/Q076 was completed. No novelty inference is made.

## Q080 follow-up

TESSCut S7 and S34 quality-zero 3×3 target-centered sky-subtracted fluxes show resolved troughs, 8.58 and 5.52 ppt core/outer median loss, respectively. These depths are sensitive to baseline slope, particularly S34. S7 has no gap greater than three cadences within ±2.5 d of the 1501.4317 screening time; S34 has a 2234.77837–2234.81309 gap at the outer ingress, with core covered. S7 core/outer counts 33/67 and S34 101/197. Difference images localize peaks at (5,5), S7 target WCS (5.393,5.139), positive centroid (4.895,5.170); S34 target (5.801,5.055), centroid (5.519,5.121). These are zero-based positions; positive centroid is a descriptive statistic, not a fitted PRF. The screen times are not fitted centers.

Gaia DR3 108-arcsec cone finds nearest neighbor G=17.992 at 28.5 arcsec. The brightest capable neighbor, Gaia 2928006547183841792, G=11.980, lies at pixel (3.71,2.88) in S7 and (4.24,2.60) in S34, well away from the losses. Gaia 2928006512824090240, G=14.689 at 46.8 arcsec, lies at (7.61,4.84) and (8.13,4.97). Both loss images favor the target's vicinity over these resolved alternatives, subject to PRF and unresolved-source limitations. Difference maps and plots are in `99243741/helper-*`, reproducible with `helper-q080.py`; Gaia response and query receipt are saved. Separate lead analysis addresses source-specific orbital timing, alternative reductions and other sectors.

Q080 measured SAP timing: robust trapezoid plus linear baseline fits at ±1.5/2/2.5 d yield S7 centers 1501.44114/1501.44149/1501.43990 and S34 2235.62783/2235.62984/2235.63754; S34 widest fit hits the ingress bound. These fit-window ranges are sensitivity checks, not uncertainties; asymmetric profiles and correlated systematics preclude claiming precision from them. The S7–S34 separation about 734.19 d is 0.22 d shorter than 22×Gaia P; 11×double that period predicts the identical separation. S88 fitted 3703.5936–3703.6075 but its egress is gapped 3703.8713–3704.1537 and profile differs, so same-kind interpretation is uncertain. Its S7 separation is about 1.07 d shorter than 66×Gaia P (equivalently 33×2P). S87 nominal fit 3670.62 is strongly baseline-dependent in depth and affected by the 3669.517–3669.735 ingress gap; it cannot establish a precise same-kind clock or eliminate the doubled-period alias. Gaia period error accumulates and source-specific phase uncertainties are correlated; do not treat nominal residual as a significance test. `helper-q080-timing.py` and `99243741/helper-timing.json` record parameters and source hashes.
