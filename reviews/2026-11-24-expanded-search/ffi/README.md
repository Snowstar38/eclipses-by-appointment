# Repeated TESS-SPOC full-frame signals

The MAST data-validation inventories cover sectors 36–39 and 57–86. After removing previously reviewed TICs, 23,931 fields had reports in at least two disjoint sectors. We ranked those fields by number of sectors, baseline and TIC, then reviewed the first 1,600. Each endpoint XML was checked for a repeated period, comparably deep primary and a difference-image offset within 12 arcsec. Of 614 repeated pairs, 12 fields reached the raw two-reduction binary shortlist. [All 1,600 outcomes](screened.csv) · [Sector phase curves for the 12](folds.png)

Six retained sources have five-sector primary timing fits and a resolved secondary or alternating unequal eclipses in raw photometry. Both the primary and secondary deficits favour the host in target-pixel fits in two sectors. The source files give MAST URLs, hashes and measurements for each sector. The independent QLP and TESS-SPOC reductions use the same spacecraft exposures. Ground surveys, where available, test the ephemeris with independent observations. The individual limitations accompany each entry in the host table.

Controlled CDS cones, SIMBAD identities and bibliographies, alias searches, both IJspeert eclipse catalogues, Villanova, ground surveys and previous project and Chopard-Vilhem identifiers were checked. HEASARC’s AAVSO catalogue distribution returned Algol as a positive control. The full IJspeert et al. (2024) eclipse table and the Prša et al. (2022) TESS catalogue yielded no source within 15 arcmin with a period matching any of the six adopted periods or their quarter, half, double or quadruple to 0.2%. The six stars had public threshold-crossing events before this work. The sample was selected for strong repeated signals and cannot estimate population incidence.
