# Independent helper audit

Representative quality-zero SAP windows for every flagged dip were independently plotted (`helper-<TIC>.png`); each used FITS header TIC matches the queue and its SHA256 matches its bulk receipt (`helper-flags.json`). These are normalized QLP SAP values, not absolute detector counts. Actual nearby quality-zero gaps are in `helper-gaps.json`.

Q001 and Q007 S74 show contemporaneous gap-associated step and recovery near BTJD 3326.9. Q003, Q004, Q006 and Q008 S94 show the same broad decrease, minimum and gap-associated recovery at BTJD 3874.9, as do Q003, Q008 and Q010 S104 near 4197. These common shapes are evidence against treating those windows as separate stellar eclipses. Q009 S95 feature terminates at gap 3894.48238–3895.81572; S96 begins at end of gap 3920.46148–3921.74618, so neither has a complete event shape. Q002 and Q006 have short-timescale modulation, and Q010 S96 windows are smooth, gap-affected trends. Q001 and Q007 other flagged windows include large steps and baseline variation.

Four downloaded TESSCut files and their retrieval/FITS SHA256 receipts are `helper-cut-receipts.json`. The WCS target positions, baseline windows and counts are `helper-diff.json`; reference and difference plots are `helper-diff-<TIC>.png`. Each target has finite positive target-area flux. Q002 s30, Q005 s32 and Q006 s87 differences peak near target; these establish approximate location of variability, not source ownership against unresolved capable neighbors. Q010 s104 difference has substantial positive-negative dipole consistent with image motion and contemporaneous common-mode behavior. Difference flux must not be interpreted as independent observing occasions.

Q005 S5, S32 and S98 show roughly similar broad, asymmetric, multicomponent troughs around the Gaia-period predictions. They suggest repeat photometric variability but do not secure discrete eclipse morphology or unique cycle count. S32 3x3 background-subtracted TESSCut reproduces raw QLP structure (`helper-Q005-aperture.png`). Using 2194.214 and 46.13746894 d as provisional reference, the additional S98 prediction 3993.575 has data only from +1.529 d; it is not a core null. The other windows and actual coverage are `helper-Q005-predictions.json/png`. No Gaia period is asserted as measured.

Q002 Gaia-period provisional S97 prediction 3968.2074 has 173 good samples in ±0.2 d; data span ±2 d but a 0.308 d gap affects the pre-baseline. Smooth ~1 ppt variation around the predicted center masks a shallow signal and the assumed initial center is uncertain, so this is not a strong sensitive null (`helper-Q002-null.json/png`).

No physical verdicts were assigned. Close-neighbor attribution, aliases and geometry remain independent questions where a stellar eclipse interpretation survives. Thanks for including me in the review.
