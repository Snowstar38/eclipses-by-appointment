# Signed-flux background investigation

Both extreme apparent dips are dominated by the pipeline background estimate, not compelling standalone evidence for an eclipse. This is a light-curve-level assessment, not a pixel localization or a moving-object identification. We did not find a surviving discrete dip that warrants a TPF follow-up on this evidence. Six original TESS-SPOC FITS files, all three available sectors for both targets, can be fetched independently with `python /path/to/fable-fold-35/background/download.py`, which constructs the public MAST TESS-SPOC HLSP URLs directly and requires no workspace cache. Run `python /path/to/fable-fold-35/background/analyze.py` after downloading; both scripts resolve paths relative to themselves and work from any directory. `analyze.py` reproduces the plots, signed cadence tables and window-median `measurements.csv`; units are electrons per second and times are BTJD. Quality-zero and nonzero samples are shown separately, not discarded. Gray bands mark the measurement windows. The vertical plot limits for TIC 218233922 intentionally clip the large positive background spike and one negative outlier; all finite samples are retained in the signed CSV.

TIC 52157743, sector 44: In the clean pre-event window 2505.50–2506.10, median SAP is 596.6 and SAP+BKG is 1500.9. In 2506.87–2506.96 SAP is -4858.5, BKG is 6716.7, and their sum is 1810.5; all 13 points in this window have nonzero quality, specifically 4096. The clean post-event window 2508.70–2509.40 has SAP 599.4 and sum 1520.9. Adding back background removes the deep depression and reveals a large bright excursion instead. The residual sum varies strongly, so it is not a clean stellar-flux measurement; no source attribution is claimed. A small gap occurs near 2508.0.

TIC 218233922, sector 42: Pre-event 2454.50–2455.10 has median SAP 1131.2 and sum 2020.0. On the descent at 2456.85–2457.00, SAP is 386.4, BKG 2077.4 and sum 2460.6; all 22 samples are quality 4096. After a gap of roughly a day, 2461.30–2461.80 has SAP -375.5, BKG 2321.4 and sum 1944.5; all 72 samples have nonzero quality. By 2463.30–2463.90 SAP is 1109.7 and sum 1903.8, all quality zero. The huge SAP depression disappears on restoring the estimated background; the restored level has secular drift and a large positive excursion, and the gap prevents testing continuity. A residual astrophysical dip cannot be ruled out in the gap, but this event does not establish one. In the other target's sector 42 light curve the same broad time interval does not show the comparable SAP drop, so we do not claim the effect is common mode.

Values are medians of individual sums, not necessarily sums of median components. SAP+BKG is an aperture-sum proxy and can contain contaminating background or sources; adding back a fitted background does not itself prove the physical mechanism. 4096 is reported as a bit value without inferring its cause. No raw-pixel or moving-object analysis was performed.
