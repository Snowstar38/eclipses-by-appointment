# Final 34: review evidence

All four accepted batches contain 34 completed reviews: 33 confirmed and one probable. Two confirmed systems, TIC 407991939 and TIC 407220461, are known catalogue recoveries and excluded from new additions. The 32 qualifying additions comprise 31 confirmed and one probable. The final paper sample contains 404 systems (376 confirmed and 28 probable), all scoped uncatalogued; 13 plausible cases in the review registers remain outside that sample. The R register contains 174 rows, including three known confirmed recoveries.

`FULL-RESULTS.csv` indexes the 34 reports and separates physical verdict, completion, novelty, and next check. The seven catalogue scopes and their bounds are recorded in `shared-catalogues`. A probable classification denotes real repeating eclipses with a missing or failed check. These bounded reviews use five data-permitted checks; independent reductions can share the same TESS photons, and existing valid evidence may be reused. They do not imply exhaustive review of every event or sector.

The project coordinator thanks the leads and helpers for their contributions. Automated measurements are attributed to their agent authors in the retained notes.

Reports, compact numeric evidence, scripts, query receipts, selected raw QLP and difference panels, and source hashes are included. FITS files, cubes, arrays, archives and bulk logs are omitted. Scripts may require configuration and retrieval of omitted data. The manifest records separate source and export SHA-256 hashes; paths and private names are sanitized. External URLs and identifiers are retained.
