# Batch 08

Three objects show prominent short-timescale SAP or corrected waves. TIC 383529972 returns a fundamental near 0.382 d in S22 and a strong half-period harmonic in S49, with continuous broad structure in both flux products. TIC 260406489 shows ~0.1373 d structure in both columns across four sectors. TIC 70889777 has ~1.406 d continuous SAP variation across two sectors, largely removed by QLP correction. TIC 321813701 and TIC 290665111 likewise show smooth, evolving variability rather than an isolated repeat at the long trial periods.

The two deepest SAP windows of TIC 94039922 remain open. They are broad depressions of different shape with nearby coverage breaks and the 22.8233 d pair hypothesis has no fully covered independent return. The S34 partial forecast misses the morphology seen in S61/S88. Several other targets share the S94 disturbance or have candidates that adjoin sector boundaries.
