Current evidence: mixed-method and faint-band reviews

Supplement 5. Current review evidence, 5 November 2026.

The 380 completed mixed-method reviews comprise 29 confirmed, 37 probable, 69 plausible, 235 refuted selected screening signals, 5 non-eclipse variables and 5 unresolved outcomes. Among the 66 physically confirmed or probable mixed-method cases, 19 are known recoveries, 1 retains a source-attribution hold, and 46 are accepted claims (18 confirmed, 28 probable). The held case is not counted.

The separate faint-band conjunction and raw-event search selected 2500 previously unreviewed Gaia spectroscopic binaries at G = 11.5–12 and P = 20–60 d; 2420 had QLP products in the archive inventory, and 2420 science targets had successfully downloaded data. Current download manifests contain 0 unavailable products; earlier failed attempts, including disk-floor deferrals, are archived separately. Unavailable data are not physical non-detections. All mapping failures, missing data and nonflags remain in the target-level registers. Visual triage and prior-identity checks led to 138 full physical reviews: 42 confirmed, 28 probable, 40 plausible and 25 refuted selected signals and 3 unresolved outcomes. These reviews contribute 66 accepted claims (42 confirmed, 24 probable), with known recoveries excluded. Sequential expansion and visual selection make these operational results unsuitable for unbiased occurrence or completeness estimates.

Physical confidence, source ownership, Gaia-orbit association and catalogue novelty are distinct. A selected-pattern refutation does not rule out every hidden binary. Reported recurrences are not necessarily unique orbital periods; blank ephemerides remain unresolved. Multiple reductions of the same exposures do not supply independent observing occasions, and missing or invalid coverage is not a null.

The first four volumes preserve dated evidence. This register and the current manuscript supersede historical inclusion labels.
