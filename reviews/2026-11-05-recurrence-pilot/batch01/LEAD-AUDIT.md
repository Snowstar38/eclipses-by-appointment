## Deciding evidence

- V007 / TIC 147413927: Seven representative SAP brightening-then-trough events at cycles 0, 2, 35, 67, 100, 101, 115 fit a 22.04395-d photometric spacing; residuals are within 0.03 d. Actual S90 q0 cores at P/2, P/3, P/4 are covered with no comparable trough (see `v007-clock.json`). Helper's independent valid TESSCut 3x3 extraction reproduces S37 and S90 deficits of 4.51/4.40 ppt. Independent difference peaks lie 0.060/0.200 pixel from the target WCS location, while nearest G16.85 neighbor contributes only ~0.00272 of target G light and cannot explain the selected depth under equal throughput. Gaia SB1 e=0.6805, P=22.03927±0.00799 d predicts one edge-on conjunction kind within 0.30–0.66 d of the trough across S37–S90; fixed-cycle linear covariance allows 0.60–1.13 d. The other conjunction kind is ~6 d off. The raw and alternative curves have a broad bright hump and smooth rounded trough, without an isolated compact ingress, egress or occultation core. Heartbeat-only modulation remains materially viable. Campaign PROBABLE requires established repeating eclipses, so the eclipse interpretation is PLAUSIBLE. No eclipse period/epoch/depth is adopted in `RESULTS.csv`; 22.04395 d is retained as a conditional photometric spacing in the numeric receipt.
- V002 / TIC 265066551: The nine-sector huge ~1.26-d waveform is not a sequence of Gaia-22.37-d eclipses. A G8.12 neighbor at 8.706 arcsec is ~18.15 times the selected target's G light. The selected target's total G disappearance would only remove ~5.2% of their combined light, well below the 30–50% aperture oscillation. Difference pixels blend both stars and do not identify the variable uniquely. This is an interesting short-period source, not a verified eclipse of the queued Gaia target.
- V001/V008/V009: S94 dips share the same fine-scale time structure in three stars, including V001/V009 on the same camera/CCD and V008 on a different camera/CCD. V001/V009 also show concurrent S96 trough, gap and reset. `cross-target-s94.png` and `cross-target-s96.png` provide direct positive common-mode evidence. V001 has further dip/overshoot pairs; V008 long ramps and steps.
- V004: S99 and S101 troughs are gap/step-bound; abrupt centroid movement and recovery track S99 4053 and S101 4107. Formal trapezoid fits of those portions are not eclipse timings.
- V003/V005/V006/V010: Direct full-sector raw inspection shows oscillatory or smooth-wave variability. Local detector dips are parts of those patterns, without a stable isolated same-kind eclipse clock. V005's broad modulation may be orbital at a Gaia-like scale, but no discrete eclipse was established.

## Scope and limitations

Raw q0 SAP full-sector plots and representative selected-window plots exist per target. `fitted-windows.json` is a provisional fit to flagged detector windows only; its singular or bound-hitting values are not precision epochs. KSPSAP is a same-photon correction and disappears from many newer FITS; helper TESSCut is a separate extraction of the same observing occasions. Gaia G flux bounds are approximate across bandpass and aperture throughput. The V007 Gaia calculation is source-specific but assumes edge-on geometry and uses fixed-cycle linear covariance. We did not perform exhaustive all-event fitting or a full stellar/heartbeat model; the identified uncertainty is reported in the V007 next check.

The helper's independent checks and qualified judgments are in `HELPER-AUDIT.md`, with pixel/neighbor/aperture receipts and scripts. The helper and lead independently resolved V007 as PLAUSIBLE for eclipses after examining the shape against the campaign definition. This lead accepts those measurements within their stated limits.
