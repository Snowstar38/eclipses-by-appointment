## Limits and next check

This is a strong target-centered recurring orbital photometric signal, but raw and alternative curves show a smooth rounded trough after a broad bright hump, without separately resolved ingress, egress or occultation core. Heartbeat-only variability is materially viable, so an eclipse is not established. KSPSAP suppresses early features; Gaia uncertainties are fixed-cycle linear approximations and bandpass/throughput affect neighbor flux bounds. Next: Obtain multicolor or high-cadence photometry, or a physical heartbeat model with an additional eclipse component, before calling this an eclipsing system.
