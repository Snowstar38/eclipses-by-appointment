# Independent helper audit — review9

Nine Gaia DR3 cone searches (60 arcsec) and one representative TESScut extraction per target were completed. Source receipts, FITS SHA256, query VOTables, individual difference panels and numerical data are in `helper-pixels/`; scripts `helper_fetch.py`, `helper_analyze.py`, and `helper_neighbors.py`. The TESScut FFI cutouts and QLP derive from the same TESS exposures: this is a different extraction of shared photons, not an independent observation. Pixel checks use quality=0, per-cadence median faintest 40% background, an eclipse core |dt|<0.3 fitted duration, and bilateral shoulders 0.75–1.75 durations from a fitted event. Positive-clipped centroids are descriptive, with no formal astrometric error; pixel scale and blending prevent them from independently excluding subpixel sources. All nine representative difference panels were visually inspected and show a deficit near the target. No noise-model calibrated source-exclusion claim is made.

Gaia neighbor tests use target and neighbor G flux ratio as a TESS proxy, optimistic full throughput, and a test depth equal to the smaller of QLP fitted central depth and 5×5 TESScut window depth. Gaia-only fluxes, unmodelled color/throughput, sources absent in Gaia, and missing G-band values limit completeness; background and total contaminant flux are approximations. Within 60 arcsec, 342060048 has a capable Gaia source 4576082942870779776, 17.52 arcsec, G=10.078, requiring approximately 11.6% intrinsic loss under these assumptions; 95623701 has 6022994340434249728, 16.81 arcsec, G=11.321, requiring approximately 82.4%. Their subpixel separations preclude confident source exclusion. No other capable Gaia G-measured source was found within 60 arcsec in the other seven cones. These intrinsic requirements are not pixel fractional losses. See `helper-pixels/conservative-neighbors.json`.

Independent arithmetic of all usable event depths and least-squares linear ephemerides was repeated in `helper_numeric.py` and `helper-numeric-audit.json`; depth differences are zero, period differences below 2e-13 d, epochs below 2e-11 d. This checks stored arithmetic, not event selection, systematics, covariance validity or alias uniqueness. Formal timing errors and conservative cadence floors are not absolute accuracy guarantees.

Gaia geometry independently reconstructed for TIC 184398115, 95623701, 342060048 and 284613090 in `helper_geometry.py` / `helper-geometry.json`. Gaia periastron time is relative to J2016.0, JD 2457389.0; BTJD zero is 2457000, giving +389 d. Edge-on conjunctions from true longitude 90° and 270°, Kepler transformation, and nearest cycle exactly reproduce the lead's values. All four are SB2; swapping omega by 180° exchanges the two conjunctions, leaving the unordered closest conjunction unchanged, and the A/B labels alone do not establish primary eclipse or component identity. Published Gaia orbital elements and their stated uncertainties are inputs; no independent radial-velocity refit is performed. Compared to Gaia, fitted same-kind photometric periods differ by −0.008998, −0.022131, +0.007609, +0.011114 d respectively; accumulated event residuals versus the Gaia period from first to last are −1.187, −2.512, +0.381, +1.044 d respectively. Gaia epoch propagation, covariances, systematics and cycle aliases matter. Consecutive-sector events constrain a single cycle for 184398115, 342060048 and 284613090; 95623701 deep events assume 112 cycles and alternatives remain to be tested. Conjunction disagreement is not by itself proof of a different source.
