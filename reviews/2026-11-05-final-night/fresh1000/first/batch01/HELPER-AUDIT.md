# Independent helper audit

I inspected all eight `helper-localization.png` images and the combined `helper-contact.png`. These are custom quality-zero TESScut extractions, independent reductions of the same TESS photons, not independent occasions. `helper-numeric-audit.json` contains exact measurements; `helper-hashes.json` seals inputs and artifacts. The population is fresh-band-next1000; lead verified raw identity and hashes. TESScut FITS and Gaia cone response hashes are in per-target JSON; 60 arcsec Gaia DR3 cones are bounded, not exhaustive or sensitive to unlisted blends. The initial Z001 ESA TAP query timed out after 80 seconds; a successful retry produced the saved response. 

Each neighbor budget assumes fully captured target plus a single fully captured neighbor, calculates idealized intrinsic eclipse loss from measured blended 3x3 aperture loss and approximate Gaia G-band flux ratio. This is not a comparison of neighbor intrinsic loss directly to blended loss. TESS color differences, spatial throughput, other stars and missing sources limit the approximation; neither the simplified flux budget nor positive-only centroid is calibrated astrometric modeling.
