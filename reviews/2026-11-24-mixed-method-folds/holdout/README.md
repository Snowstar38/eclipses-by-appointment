# Other TESS light-curve extractions

The MAST inventory returned 61 QLP, 43 TESS-SPOC and two SPOC light curves. We downloaded all 45 non-QLP products, verified target and sector headers, and tested their original fold predictions. Most products overlap the QLP observations and offer a different aperture and reduction on the same telescope. Two products belong to special sector 1751, which observed the interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS field. Their observing intervals contain no predicted core for these two targets. [Archive inventory](archive.json) · [Input hashes and coverage](products.csv) · [NASA sector release notes](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/tess/data_release_notes.html)

On quality-zero samples, the core is the central 70% of the original fold width. Each shoulder extends from 0.8 to 2 widths away. Each zone needs at least three finite measurements and at least 55% of nominal cadence coverage. The uncertainty scale uses local scatter, reduced assuming blocks of three, and half the disagreement of the two baseline medians. A recovery needs both aperture and corrected loss to exceed five uncertainty scales and 30% of the original box depth. An empty window has both upper three-scale depths below 30% of the original depth. A shallow noisy dip can fall between these criteria. There were 15 insufficiently covered, six sensitive empty and five indeterminate product windows. Counts are per product, so duplicate observations are possible.
