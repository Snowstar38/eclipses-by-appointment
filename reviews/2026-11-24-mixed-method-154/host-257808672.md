# R1867 · TIC 257808672

TIC 257808672, Gaia DR3 1786928913098116864, is a confirmed eclipsing source at RA 321.94430875774424 deg and Dec 19.11833627703276 deg. The primary ephemeris is **3.19798973 d** and **2802.5393100 BTJD**. The median fitted primary FWHM is 0.17026 d. The signal entered the review in the aperture of TIC 257808668, about 37 arcsec away from the emitting source. The catalogue entry and sky-map point use TIC 257808672.

## TESS measurements

A Gaussian-plus-linear fit to eight SAP minima in sectors 55 and 82 has timing residuals within 0.0021 d over 235 cycles. The fitted clock places a primary dip in all 16 covered predictions, including eight events withheld from that fit. SAP median-core depths range from 6.02 to 7.45 ppt in the blended QLP aperture, with median 6.710 ppt. Corrected depths range from 3.54 to 7.48 ppt. Thirteen well-covered half-phase windows show smaller SAP losses of 1.55–2.58 ppt and corrected losses of 0.75–1.79 ppt. The unequal primary and secondary phases support the full photometric period.

TESSCut difference frames and separated flux templates place both event families on the same neighboring star. Per-sector median primary centroids are 0.75 and 1.55 arcsec from the host and 36.60 and 37.47 arcsec from the nominated target. Secondary medians are 1.78 and 2.09 arcsec from the host. Template-fit primary losses are 17.1 and 17.9 electrons per second, with secondary losses of 6.6 and 6.4 electrons per second. These are detector-flux differences. The quiescent flux of the fainter star is model-sensitive in the blended scene, so these losses do not establish fractional depths for the resolved binary.

Broad modulation changes between sectors and is compatible with the earlier activity classifications. A two-harmonic model fitted outside both dip windows leaves negative residuals in both cores in each sector. The sharply bounded primary and repeatable unequal secondary support eclipses superposed on that broader variability. ## Independent ZTF data

Resolved ZTF photometry reproduces both phases in both filters at the fixed TESS period and epoch without a ground period or phase search. The primary contrasts are 302 mmag in g (cycle-bootstrap 95% interval 294–388, 13 sampled cycles) and 221 mmag in r (207–247, 22 cycles). The secondary contrasts are 44 mmag in g (30–72, 20 cycles) and 46 mmag in r (33–60, 28 cycles). The intervals condition on the broad out-of-eclipse fits and the fixed photometric clock. ## Earlier reports and scope

ASAS-SN catalogued this object as ROT at 3.1926 d, ZTF as RSCVN at 3.1942271 d, and Gaia DR3 as RS. Kostov et al. (2025) included TIC 257808672 in their Table 2 of unvetted machine-selected eclipse candidates. The contribution here is validation of two eclipse phases and their source attribution.
