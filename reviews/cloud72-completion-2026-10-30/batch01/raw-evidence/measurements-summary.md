# Batch 01 measurement receipts

All 30 QLP FITS TICID and SECTOR headers matched the expected identities; per-file SHA-256 in identity-checks.json and receipt-manifest.json. Search-derived cycle counts condition the representative raw SAP event ephemerides in raw-events.json; event plots are raw-event-panels.png. Fits do not integrate exposures or provide formal period uncertainties. Nine targets have repeated localized events. TIC 10790605 has no event passing the fit quality filter; its four-harmonic diagnostic (10790605/harmonic-test.json) does not establish a narrow eclipse. Harmonics may absorb broad eclipses and its iid errors ignore correlated noise.

TIC 465073693 has unequal alternating local depths, about 12–14% at primary and 5.6–6.5% at phase 0.5 over sectors 10, 63 and 99 (465073693/alias-check.json), supporting a full primary recurrence of 6.27226 d over the half-period alias; other aliases remain conditional on cycle numbering.

TESS-SPOC sector 14 independently reproduces two TIC 441741446 dips at roughly 1%; its identity, URL, SHA-256 and fixed-shape measurements are in independent-results.json. For the other nine, inventory returned no matching same-sector light curve or timed out. TESScut custom aperture extracts provide an independently processed check but share TESS pixels, not an independent observation or archive pipeline.

TESScut cutouts, signed difference images, per-event in/pre/post counts and headers for two sectors of each of nine repeat-event targets are in tesscut-downloads.json, pixel-results.json, and per-target pixel_sNNN.npz/png files. The green plus is the target and white crosses are TIC neighbours theoretically capable of producing the measured 3x3 dip in an ideal total eclipse (neighbour-results.json). TIC cone is 60 arcsec; Tmag-based limits neglect throughput and colour uncertainty. No TESScut test was attempted for 10790605 because no qualifying event template was found. The positive-clipped centroid in pixel-results.json is biased toward the target by its 5x5 measurement window and has no calibrated uncertainty; inspect signed maps instead.

source-model-comparison.json compares target and each capable TIC neighbour inside 30 arcsec using the same constant-plus-circular-Gaussian PSF, with width fitted to the blended out image. This is a bounded model sensitivity test, not a calibrated likelihood or significance: real PSFs are asymmetric, noise is correlated and source blends bias width. Candidate target fits have smaller RSS than close neighbours in both sectors; TIC 465263741 S10 discrimination is weak (neighbour/target RSS ratios 1.32–1.70), with S38 ratios 2.27–4.40. Unresolved or uncatalogued stars remain possible. Ratios for the nearest capable neighbours are 20.68/6.04 for TIC 23112764, 12.72/3.41 for 316405449, 16.97/9.26 for 300903537, and 11.78/12.59 for 280075497.

Gaia DR3 90-arcsec cone rows for the first seven targets were reused from the original VizieR I/355 query (maximum 200 rows); provenance, SHA-256 and rows are in gaia-dr3-reused.json. Fresh Gaia DR3 cones succeeded for 23112764, 300903537, 280075497, 276514049 and 10790605 as gaia-cone.json; the other fresh requests timed out. The TIC cone and Gaia source lists are separate; Gaia G and TIC T feasibility are not interchangeable. Cone or TIC catalogue completeness and unidentified close companions are limitations.

Results are evidence, not verdicts. The review lead must decide morphology, source attribution, independent-pipeline coverage, geometry and final classification. No source table, paper or external publication was modified.
