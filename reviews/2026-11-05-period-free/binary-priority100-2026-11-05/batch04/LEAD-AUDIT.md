# Batch04 lead audit - T031-T040

All ten fixed queue identities were reviewed. The source packet for every TIC and every QLP FITS used by `lead_measure.py` matched its saved SHA-256. I inspected the retained raw SAP windows and full-sector context, actual cadence gaps, current MAST footprint availability, Gaia orbital conjunctions with each target's NSS covariance, and the shared 120-arcsec neighbor catalogue. Terra04 independently inspected representative raw windows and source pixels, including valid TESScut alternatives for T033 and T037 and an invalid cropped cutout for T036. The helper's report is `helper-audit.md`. No detector score or Gaia period was converted directly into a physical verdict.

The result is eight REFUTED selected eclipse interpretations and two PLAUSIBLE cases, all bounded reviews COMPLETE. No target meets CONFIRMED or PROBABLE criteria. Novelty is UNKNOWN for all ten because four shared VizieR gates remain unavailable despite valid checks of other groups. These are review outcomes for a priority-selected queue, not a yield or occurrence estimate and not a claim that any refuted star cannot be binary.

T033 is the principal credible candidate. The s80 ~6-8 ppt flat-bottomed deficit appears in raw QLP SAP and a target-containing independent TESScut aperture extraction. s91 starts low at the +5 Gaia-cycle phase and recovers; TESScut apertures also show this partial occasion, but no s91 ingress or pre-event baseline exists. The conditional integer aliases n=6 and 9 have covered flat s92 cores; n=8 has substantial partial flat-core coverage (first offset -0.383 d, not the full +/-0.6 d interval) and is disfavored under the assumed phase and shape; n=7 is gapped and n=3, 4 and 5 remain viable. The photometric period is not uniquely measured. The s80/s91 coarse pixel deficits peak near the target, but the crowded field, including an 18.8 arcsec G=14.89 neighbor, limits attribution. The current footprint lists only cached sectors 80, 91 and 92. PLAUSIBLE preserves that uncertainty.

T036 s85 ~3625 has a broad raw trough and other unlike/gap-intersecting troughs, with no secure same-kind 54.23 d repeat. The quick TESScut is half zero-valued, invalidating its low-pixel background estimator; it cannot serve as a null. PLAUSIBLE retains the possible single event. T037's s73 trough coincides with a 0.35 d gap and ~21 ppt step; target-containing 3x3/5x5 TESScut extractions lack the QLP trough while the field background changes broadly. That combination positively supports a background/extraction false flag, with the approximate background model recorded as a limit.

T032/T035/T038/T039 share a broad s94 ~3875 disturbance despite different targets and Gaia periods; their individual raw panels show the actual ~0.25 d interruption, sloping baselines and quality-flagged context. T031 s25 has shallow irregular baseline fluctuations and s86 has much larger ramps/steps. T034 s81 is split into persistent flux branches; T040 s29 sits on a flare-like excursion and s96 on a gap/reset. Those positive raw-shape observations support the remaining refutations. Time spacing or common-mode counts alone were not used as verdicts.

The source packet and bulk-window hashes, every QLP FITS source hash, shared Gaia/queue hashes, helper fetch receipts and output files are frozen in `SOURCE-HASHES.json`, `helper-source-hashes.json` and `SHA256SUMS.json`. `RESULTS.csv` separates completion, verdict and novelty. Scripts in this folder reproduce the numeric and report artifacts. No paper, shared register, other batch or external application was edited.
