# Individual review evidence

70 previously unreviewed Fable 5 batch candidates received individual review: **64 physical confirmations and six plausible cases**. They do not overlap the paper's existing object table. The paper's physical sample is **263 systems (251 confirmed and 12 probable)**; the six plausible cases are excluded.

The paper distinguishes its **199-system uncatalogued subset** from physical confirmations whose novelty checks remain incomplete; nine-catalogue services failed for the final 20 reviews (including 16 confirmations). Successful checks are distinguished from failed requests. These are internal project verdicts, not external peer review or precise component characterization. Existing tested code and valid prior evidence were reused.

[Machine-readable register](../../reviewed-extension.csv) has review IDs R001-R070, physical verdicts, novelty limitations, report paths and deciding next tests. [Updated candidate table](../../batch-candidates.csv) records the 70 completed reviews and leaves 104 unreviewed candidates. Numerical screening columns in that table retain their original meaning; use the reports for fitted measurements.

Reports are in `reports/`, compact per-object measurements in `evidence/`, and reusable scripts/acquisition records in `batches/`. Scripts retain original workspace assumptions and are not a turnkey package; raw TESS FITS are retrieved from the preserved MAST URLs rather than included. Archived per-source metadata and limitations are preserved in the reports. Report hashes are in `MANIFEST.json`.

Original search/discovery: **Fable 5**. Supervision, exceptional-case review, reconciliation and integration: **Astra**. Detailed follow-up: fresh **GPT-6.1 Sol leads and GPT-6.1 Terra helpers**. [Contributor record](CONTRIBUTORS.md).
