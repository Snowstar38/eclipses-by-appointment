# Independent helper audit, batch 03

Scope: independently inspected the harmonic diagnostic and representative raw windows for TIC 314041837, the anomalous windows of TIC 343926224, both alias cases, and the target/neighbor pixel evidence for TIC 62940616. Recomputed every timing slope, uncertainty, and epoch from stored measured times, not historical predictions. Checked the capable-neighbor lists in all ten difference-image JSON files. This is an audit of selected saved data, not an all-sector extraction or Monte Carlo.

- TIC 314041837: S77 phase plot shows a broad double wave with a localized, substantially sharper primary core and a second localized depression near the opposite minimum. The two-harmonic model fit outside the primary core leaves a central residual of -30.31 ppt, against 4.72 ppt outer residual scatter. This supports eclipse-like structure superposed on smooth modulation, without proving a unique physical model: the exclusion fit is descriptive and its scatter is not a calibrated significance. The 0.783 d local trapezoid width hits the upper fitting bound in most S77/S85 events and absorbs modulation, so it is not a physical eclipse duration; depth also depends on that baseline. The period is a local-model timing measurement, subject to morphology systematics.
- TIC 62940616: four S46 events in the saved difference stack yield a compact peak at the target pixel. The deficit centroid (7.7875, 7.2358) is 0.205 pixel from the target (7.6350, 7.0985) and about 1.33 pixels from capable Gaia 3600574305187842176 (6.6235, 6.5892), displaced away from that neighbor. The target-centered 1/3/5-pixel-square depths are 47.8/44.6/42.1 ppt. The neighbor is 22.67 arcsec away and a G-band flux-budget estimate requires approximately 0.329 fractional eclipse if it alone causes the signal; bandpass, dilution and source blending limit the estimate. Its pixel's 0.0416 fractional loss is blended pixel light, not a measurement of that star's intrinsic eclipse. Evidence favors the target at TESS resolution, with resolved follow-up desirable.
- TIC 343926224: inspected raw-events.png. S76 cycle 154 has a large structured rise immediately before a shallow fitted core, and S84 cycle 176 has a gap and broad baseline distortion with a poor trapezoid fit. Excluding these two from precise timing has positive profile-based justification; they remain documented as observed windows, not clean nulls. Five other measured events give P=10.7289779063 d. Their reduced chi-square is 18.08, so the error is inflated by sqrt(18.08); residual systematics remain a limitation.
- TIC 397178704 and TIC 7446763: selected odd historical cycles are the deeper group. Across all fitted events, even/odd median depths are 160.21/175.67 ppt and 35.84/72.08 ppt respectively. Their six and twelve selected same-kind times independently reproduce orbital periods 11.0096850472 d and 5.9119066682 d. Representative raw plots and alternating depths support the doubled event-spacing interpretation; durations and depths still have local fit limitations.
- Independent centered weighted regression reproduces all ten stored periods within 3.6e-13 d, and matches their reported errors and epochs to numerical precision. Inputs use max(formal center error, half cadence), with covariance inflated for reduced chi-square above one; this is not calibrated coverage. Full numerical checks, counts, neighbor IDs and SHA-256 receipts are in helper/audit-numeric.json; executable calculation is helper/audit.py. The stored candidate lists are a G-band screening budget, not a substitute for spatial attribution or a proof of neighbor completeness.
