TIC 123220066 has one measured center in each S8, S35, S62, S89 and S99: 1526.65443, 2263.47321, 3000.23826, 3737.02556, 4066.60712 BTJD. These windows reject an equal-depth half-period recurrence under the tested ephemeris, not a much shallower secondary. The 0.04 d interior tolerance is heuristic; Gaia is an orbital prior, not an independent timing measurement.
