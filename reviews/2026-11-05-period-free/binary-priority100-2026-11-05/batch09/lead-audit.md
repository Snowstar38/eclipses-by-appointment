# Batch09 lead audit

The ten queue identities T081-T090 were retained exactly. I inspected representative raw SAP profiles and numeric timing for every target, checked FITS SHA-256 against the source packets, compared Gaia orbital periods and available covariance, and read the prior target-specific reports before deciding novelty or attribution. The helper independently extracted all ten selected windows from TESSCut, checked positive target flux/background, pixel differences, and a second T088 occasion. Their report and source receipts are retained here.

T081, T082 and T090 have real repeated short-period eclipses, but their clocks fail association with the selecting Gaia SB2 orbit. They are probable by-catch, not refutations of the eclipse signals. T085 is a real neighbor EB positively assigned to HJ 68 B by a previous stacked PSF/deblending review and is refuted only as an eclipse of the queued target. T083 has real on-target repeated B eclipses, with shorter within-sector aliases tested, but older sectors fail the same ephemeris; it remains probable rather than confirmed. T084 has two plausible events and an explicit alias check; the Gaia-near s72 prediction is gapped. T088 has two dissimilar events without a same-kind clock or unique source. T086 is an exact-ID known Chopard eclipse recovery. T087 has compatible Gaia geometry recovered from Thiele-Innes coefficients and passes the bounded half-period check; it is physically confirmed with catalogue novelty unknown. T089 satisfies representative repeated raw, timing, target pixel, alternative extraction and Gaia-covariance conjunction checks, and its half-period alternative has covered flat windows; it is physically confirmed with catalogue novelty unknown.

The actual half-period coverage calculation is in lead-half-alias.json. The AstroSpectroSB1 orientation inversion and C/H branch check are in lead-thiele.json. Conditional formal timing errors in lead-timing.json are not calibrated uncertainties on all systematics. No universal period-sigma cut was applied. The current footprint check was bounded; a footprint listing alone is not an analyzed light curve. This batch did not alter the paper, ledger or other batches.

Full source-specific Gaia NSS covariance propagation for T086 and T087 is in lead-thiele-covariance.json. Their conjunction offsets are +0.064 +/- 0.177 d and -0.193 +/- 0.197 d (0.36 and 0.98 sigma), respectively.
