# TIC 438089101: temperature and tidal scaling

The [independent audit](independent-audit/AUDIT.md) gives the current qualified interpretation. Four broad-band colors favor a conditional photometric solution near 7,100 K and E(B-V)~0.15. Accounting for shared Ks photometric covariance gives a formal profile range roughly 6,800-7,700 K; stellar-model, passband and extinction-law systematics are additional and uncalibrated. This is not a spectroscopic temperature. Nearby-field extinctions support, but do not determine, the target reddening.

With illustrative M1=2.0 solar masses and R1=3.65 solar radii, the tidal scale discrepancy is about1.7 rather than4.4 using TIC-based parameters. Its residual uncertainty is not quantitatively closed. Secondary mass~0.52 and separation~16.3 solar radii are conditional on adopted primary mass and inclination. The original color grid ends at10,700 K and cannot rigorously rule out12,000 K; the claimed7000+/-350 K symmetric precision is not adopted.

The [original source note](source/NOTE.md) is retained as an attributed analysis, including stronger claims that the audit qualifies. Its measurements and scripts are preserved for provenance, with local workspace paths redacted in exports. The intrinsic dwarf color table is available from the [Pecaut-Mamajek sequence](https://github.com/emamajek/SpectralType/blob/master/EEM_dwarf_UBVIJHK_colors_Teff.txt); the source calculation used v2022.04.16.

The physical heartbeat and tidal-oscillation verdicts do not change. Beaming remains withdrawn because of aperture dependence and sector instability, regardless of the adopted temperature factor.
