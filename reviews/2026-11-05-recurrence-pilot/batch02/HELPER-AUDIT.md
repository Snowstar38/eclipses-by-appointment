# Independent helper audit, V011–V020

Inspected the lead's ranked raw quality-zero SAP plots for all ten. These are bounded morphology reviews, not a search for every possible signal. Predictions are screening windows, not measured centers. The judgments below concern the selected feature; they do not refute the underlying spectroscopic binarity.

- V011: S94 feature BTJD 3874.897 shows broad decline and noisy trough before a gap, abrupt restoration afterward; S87 is likewise gap-adjacent, S97/96 show offsets. V014 S94 also dips before the same gap near 3874.938; shared timing and similar shape support systematic origin, with common-mode counts only corroborative.
- V012: S59 starts after a gap with a deep recovering offset; short windows show continuous fast oscillations, not isolated repeated eclipse morphology.
- V013: S28 broad smooth trough lacks clear contacts; S95 gap and offsets, other troughs/trends are not sufficient to establish repeated eclipses.
- V014: S94 shared trough and gap above; S102 is a step across a gap; S104 also trends. S13 has a sparsely sampled trough and merits qualification, not automatic identification as repeated eclipse.
- V015: S73 and S52 are continuous waves with adjacent troughs; S19 and other windows show similar broad activity. Recurrence alone does not prove eclipses.
- V016: repeated rapid ~0.2 d waveform. Independent S88 TESSCut pixel regression at half the VSX neighbor EW period gives amplitude peak (7,5), versus target (7.92,7.34) and neighbor (7.38,4.81); strongly supports neighbor contamination of that clock. The VSX receipt identifies WISE J075144.4-611237 49.46 arcsec away, EW period 0.406024 d. This does not exclude an unrelated target eclipse.
- V017: S50 rapid smooth waveform (QLP exploratory 0.279419 d), S23 a gap/offset. Correct-clock pixel amplitude peaks at (9,8), 1.78 pixels from target (7.22,7.96), and a bright column may contaminate. This offset raises an ownership issue but is insufficient alone to establish source. A cone attempt returned empty RESOURCE and is not a Gaia nonmatch. An earlier exploratory 0.4666 d map measured a different weak component and was overwritten; do not use it for the QLP signal. Insecure morphology and ownership remain.
- V018: S31/S34/S64 show broad smooth changing waves and S96 is gap-associated; no clear repeated eclipse morphology.
- V019: S18/S58/S78 show smooth rapid evolving modulation with asymmetry; catalog ROT information may explain variability but cannot alone settle source ownership.
- V020: S12/S67/S93 show rapid continuous variability, often multiple troughs in a two-day window; no established long-period eclipse from the displayed flags.

TESSCut S88 and S50 were fetched from MAST with 15x15 px centered on the target. `helper-pixel-receipt.json` records the time ranges, WCS locations, regression assumptions and SHA256 of the downloaded FITS; `helper-v016-spatial.py` and `helper-v017-spatial.py` recreate the amplitude maps. Target positions are astrometric WCS, not fitted PSF centroids. Source ownership and geometry remain distinct from morphology; neither helper pixel check supplies conjunction geometry or a sensitive long-period null. The plots and FITS use the same photons as the primary sector and do not create independent observing occasions. Full-review completion must be recorded independently from physical verdict, and novelty comes from exact identity and catalog gate receipts, not these morphology checks.
