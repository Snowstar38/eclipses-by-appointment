Period-free screening and complementary leads: reviewed evidence

Supplement 5. Evidence register, 5 November 2026.

This register covers a mixed harvest: period-free dip and brightening searches plus period-fold-only and near-threshold fold leads. The initial stratified 100 contained 30 cross-method dip, 20 other multi-sector dip, 20 single-sector dip, 10 fold-only, 10 near-threshold fold and 10 brightening targets. The subsequent 250 prioritized dip leads. Dip-detector thresholds apply only to that detector, not uniformly to the inclusive harvest.

The 350 selected reviews comprise 28 confirmed, 36 probable, 65 plausible, 211 rejected screening signals, 5 non-eclipse variables and 5 unresolved cases. Eight systems (one confirmed, seven probable) passed catalogue and claim-eligibility review and are included as R725-R732. Among the confirmed/probable outcomes, 13 are known recoveries, 43 have unknown novelty and eight have accepted scoped nonmatches; their source and orbit limitations remain.

Physical confidence, source ownership, association with the Gaia orbit and catalogue novelty are distinct assessments. A rejected signal does not rule out all possible binary interpretations. A period below is a reported recurrence, not necessarily an orbital period; blank periods remain unresolved. Report links carry deciding evidence, error meanings and limitations. Alternative reductions of the same exposures are not separate observing occasions, and missing coverage is not a null detection.

The initial stratified 100 reviews informed prioritization of a further 250. The outcome-informed selection and unequal sampling preclude population-rate estimates or independent validation of that ranking. The inclusive harvest had 2,984 leads; 80 historical exclusions left 2,904 eligible targets, and 2,554 remain outside these reviews.

The separate pre-existing catalogue reconciliation changes the counted total from 917 to 913: 17 prior-known identities are removed from the discovery count, 13 already accepted additions are included, and A74 changes from probable to confirmed. The resulting count is 807 confirmed and 106 probable. The subsequent acceptance of eight reviewed dip systems raises the total to 921: 808 confirmed and 113 probable. The 43 unresolved novelty checks do not increase the count.
