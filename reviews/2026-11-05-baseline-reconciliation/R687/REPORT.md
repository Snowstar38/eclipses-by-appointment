The Gaia catalogued period error alone projects to about **2.70 d** across 65 cycles and **1.83 d** across 44 cycles by simple multiplication, without Gaia covariance or a photometric period constraint. These are conditional central forecasts, not precise phase predictions. The half-cadence floors above are reporting allowances, not total uncertainty; pulsations and local model systematics remain unquantified.

The s7 complete box has resolved ingress, bottom and egress. TESS-SPOC SAP/PDC and Gaia-ID-targeted TGLC PSF/aperture products reproduce the s7 B-like box and A-like terminal ingress, with aperture-dependent depths. These are different reductions of the **same TESS photons**, not independent observing occasions. The s7 TESScut difference image places the B deficit about **5.2 arcsec** from the Gaia target. My s88 9×9-pixel cutout difference image puts the A deficit **2.44 arcsec** from the target (173 core and 363 flank quality-zero cadences). Both offsets are exploratory with no calibrated localization error. The 60-arcsec Gaia cone has no capable bright neighbor at the measured target-centered deficits: the G16.27 source 2.4 arcsec away has only about 1.3% of target G-band flux and cannot alone cause the observed 5.7% and 13.7% blended losses. Cross-band flux and aperture effects remain a caveat.

The Gaia high-eccentricity orbit allows unequal conjunction spacing; s7 B and s88 A are consistent with its broad predicted windows. The sector-8 and sector-34 **central** B forecasts each have zero quality-zero cadences within a nominal 0.23-day event window, so they are not clean nulls either. The s7 B and s88 A events are opposite kinds; they do **not** constitute a same-kind repeat or fix a photometric period.
