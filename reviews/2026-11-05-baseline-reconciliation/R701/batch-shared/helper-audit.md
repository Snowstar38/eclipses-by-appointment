# Independent helper evidence

Ten TESSCut 11x11 cutouts, one representative usable event per target, were retrieved with at most three concurrent requests. Each raw HTTP body, headers, URL and SHA-256 and extracted FITS hash are retained in helper-pixels/<TIC>/receipt.json. The Gaia query is a single 60-arcsec OR-cone batch; the returned VOTable indicates QUERY_STATUS OK and its full body and HTTP receipt are retained. Gaia source magnitudes are a proxy for the TESS passband and the two-source flux calculation ignores throughput, unrelated stars, and undetected sources.

helper-pixels/final-neighbors.json retains the conservative minimum positive depth among selected QLP and 1x1, 3x3, 5x5 cutout aperture estimates and every Gaia source whose total eclipse could supply that depth in the two-source Gaia-G proxy. Intrinsic eclipse fraction is distinct from the blended aperture flux deficit. Proper motion, passband, aperture response and catalogue completeness limit the test. The original difference.png labels neighbors capable at QLP median depth; final-neighbors.json can include additional sources at the conservative depth, notably 121277143.

Background is the median of the 48 lowest of 121 pixels for each cadence, core is |t-mid| < 0.3 duration and symmetric shoulder intervals cover 0.75–1.75 duration. The plotted difference image is the mean of shoulder median images minus the in-eclipse median image. Aperture time-series depths and difference-image summed depths differ because median and sum do not commute. All are independent aperture reductions of the same TESS photons as QLP, not independent observations. Centroids use positive difference pixels in a target-centered mask; they are coarse, uncalibrated localization indicators and do not exclude neighboring stars.

All ten differences peak near the target at image resolution. TIC 431589019 is especially blended: 1x1/3x3/5x5 time-series deficits are 45.0/28.4/17.1 ppt; two G-capable sources lie 37 and 50 arcsec away. Retain source ambiguity despite a target-like difference peak. TIC 121277143 has a borderline G-capable source at 34.6 arcsec, requiring 97.5% intrinsic eclipse under the simplified conservative budget. Other six-neighbor details are in final-neighbors.json.

helper-pixels/exceptions.png shows the repeatable ~5% secondary-like dips near but displaced from half phase in TIC 207780183, and flagged central samples at the TIC 121277143 sector 53 dip. The latter is not a clean timing or a clean null. helper-numeric-geometry-audit.json independently reproduces weighted period/epoch, fitted-event realized depths, and approximate Gaia conjunctions, subject to the same underlying assumptions.
