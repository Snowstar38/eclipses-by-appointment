# Batch10 lead audit: T091-T100


T091 is a **known recovery** despite four unavailable VizieR gates: Tarrants-Mendes Table 2 identifies the exact Gaia source as an EB and the historical catalogue records retired TOI-1897 signals. The raw deep and shallow eclipse kinds recur at a measured ~48.534-d clock. A P/2 alias would predict another ~54-ppt deep eclipse at BTJD 2025.2678 in sector 26; 12 uninterrupted raw core cadences cover 93% of the expected duration and show only 1.0 ppt, with a complete right baseline and 62% left baseline. `lead-t091-half-null.json` and its raw plot document this targeted deep-event exclusion. It must not count as a novel system.

T092 has two unequal sector-81 target-favored events, one of each likely kind, but no same-kind repeat; sector-54 retained dips are sloping variability. The brighter neighbor 35.8 arcsec away is resolved by the positive difference moment being 0.047 pixel from the target and 1.65 pixels from that neighbor, with the caveat that this is not a calibrated PRF fit. The period remains unknown: PLAUSIBLE.

T093 has two precise deep centers and an earlier partial raw low state plus a separate shallow eclipse. The 13-cycle ~56.251-d period is conditional: neighboring 12/14-cycle aliases lack sensitive intervening windows. The opposite-kind event cannot force a half phase without assuming Gaia's low eccentricity and source association. The shorter 39/65-cycle harmonics have visually checked flat raw windows. The independent custom aperture confirms the long eclipse that QLP KSPSAP suppresses: PLAUSIBLE, no adopted orbital period.

T094 has two clean similar ~46-ppt events but every tested 12-35-cycle alias lacks a sensitive predicted window in the available sectors; the third same-kind event is absent: PLAUSIBLE. Current Tesscut sectors are 42, 43, 70, all included in the cached coverage audit. T095 has two deep occasions and one opposite-kind event near the Gaia period. Its 72-cycle short harmonic has a visually checked flat predicted deep window at BTJD 2631.0388, but neighboring 23/25-cycle counts remain untested by sensitive windows; the opposite-kind event does not fix the phase in an eccentric orbit. Its shallow event is 3.47 Gaia linear-sigma from nominal opposite conjunction. PLAUSIBLE, no adopted period.

T096 has deep and shallow kinds each repeating and a three-center 34.108492-d deep clock: CONFIRMED. T097's two ~225-ppt primaries and a gapped later opposite-kind event favor the Gaia-near 59.67-d alias, but neighboring 11/13-cycle counts lack sensitive nulls. Shorter 36/48/60-cycle aliases have covered flat windows. The additional sector-62 cutout has negative median target flux at the WCS pixel, so it cannot provide a null. PLAUSIBLE, no adopted period. T098 has two repeats of each kind and independent 34.78577/34.78698-d kind-specific clocks and an adopted 34.786 +/- 0.002-d conservative clock; P/2 predicts a visually flat deep event at BTJD 1812.8054: CONFIRMED. T099 has three deep and two shallow events. Independent deep and shallow pairs give 28.79062 and 28.79109 d; a sector-73 deep center differs from their combined prediction by 0.041 d. Its ingress, core and egress are sampled, with a 0.220-d local gap after egress. The adopted 28.791 +/- 0.001 d uses a conservative 0.05-d third-center allowance: CONFIRMED.

T100 has many narrow eclipses across four TESS sectors. Raw folds show near-equal dips at both half phases of 0.67918 d; the supported photometric dip interval is about 0.3396 d, while orbital 0.3396 versus 0.6792 d is ambiguous. The custom aperture and difference moment favor the target, but either clock is separate from the Gaia 58.8274-d SB1 orbit: PROBABLE by-catch, no adopted orbital period or formal error.

For T091, T096, T098 and T099, `lead-event-geometry.json` compares actual measured eclipse centers with source-specific Gaia NSS covariance. All selected kinds and occasions fall within 0.05-1.5 propagated Gaia sigma of their nearest predicted conjunction. The earlier single screening phase in `lead-gaia-geometry.json` is retained only as an exploratory primitive. Harmonic checks include the two-kind distinction for T091/T099 and visually checked predicted deep nulls for T096/T098. Gaia geometry supports those four associations but is not used to invent a measured period for T093/T095/T097.

The novelty status for T092-T100 is UNKNOWN, not a negative match.
