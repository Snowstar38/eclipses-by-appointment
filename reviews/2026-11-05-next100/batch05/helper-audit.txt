Independent helper audit, Q041-Q050 (focused deciding cases; not a verdict)

Inputs and reproducibility: helper-pixel-audit.py and helper-apertures.py read lead TESSCut ZIPs and lead-pixels.json. SHA-256 for every used ZIP is recorded in their respective JSON outputs. helper-null-coverage.py reads lead-context.json and cached FITS; SHA-256 for each used source FITS is recorded in helper-null-coverage.json. The selected lead local-depth results are independent inputs and their scripts are lead_alias.py. Coverage analysis checks quality zero, finite time and positive finite SAP flux and reports core and both baseline endpoints and maximum gaps. The coverage audit evaluates only the selected hypotheses, not every possible alias. No full verdict is asserted here.

All six cutouts: quality-zero selected side sky pixels are finite with zero fraction exactly zero. Sky minima range 84.5 to 225.8 counts, and median sky-subtracted 3x3 target-aperture side flux is positive (3703 to 39053 counts), with all individual side cadence apertures positive. This rules out zero edge pixels and near-zero target flux for the selected masks, not all possible background systematic errors.

Q041 S94: centered 3x3, 5x5, 7x7, 9x9 baseline-minus-core flux is 218, 72.8, 33.6, 15.6 counts respectively, equivalent to 5.58, 1.50, 0.65, 0.30 ppt. The 5x5 difference matrix shows adjacent +130,+142 and -88,-73,-49 pixels. Together with shared Q049 event timing, this favors an instrumental flux redistribution for this flag; it says nothing by itself about the separate S67/S101 shallow events.

Q043: S18 and S58 target-relative difference-centroid offsets 0.041 and 0.126 pixel; capable Gaia neighbor at 39.0 arcsec is 0.127 times target G flux. Localization supports target association but difference centroid is not full PRF modeling. S58 predicted P/2 at 2899.0701 has 39 core and 58 points on each side, all three maximum internal gaps 0.0023 d and endpoint offsets at most 0.002 d. Lead measured local depth 0.19 ppt vs ~7–8 ppt event. Same window also tests P/4. P/3 S18 1796.3066 has core 5, sides 6 each, all max gap 0.0208 d and lead local depth 0.30 ppt. These test same-kind shorter-period aliases under the adopted ephemeris and event width, not arbitrary longer aliases or a separate eclipse kind.

Q044: S89 difference centroid 0.091 pixel from target. S88 predicted P/2/P/4 at 3708.055 has 121 core, 182 on each side, maximum internal gaps 0.0023 d and endpoint offsets <=0.0012 d, lead depth approximately zero versus ~10 ppt event. S61 P/3 at 2978.736 has 121 core and 181 each side, max gaps 0.0023 d, endpoint offsets <=0.0023 d, local depth 0.64 ppt. S61 P/2 at 2982.636 has a 0.165 d missing end of left baseline; prefer S88 null.

Q046 S65: losses decrease 15.27,10.47,9.31 ppt in 3x3,5x5,7x7, while a Gaia source 39.4 arcsec away is 1.30 times target G flux; central difference is concentrated near target, but ownership and nature of event require source-specific interpretation. This measurement is one occasion, regardless of how many extractions use these photons.

Q050 S88: 3x3 and 5x5 losses 19.08 and 19.81 ppt, then 10.49 and 10.26 ppt in 7x7 and 9x9 as additional starlight enters. Gaia G10.85 source at 66.8 arcsec has G flux ratio 1.61; brighter-source dilution is consistent with central variability, but target vs Gaia G15.45 source only 6.5 arcsec away and 0.023 G flux ratio remains unresolved at TESS pixel scale. Flux ratios are bandpass-dependent, and the close source can in principle produce a large blended eclipse.

Other queue members were not independently remeasured in this focused audit. Precise timestamps here are predictions used for coverage, not fitted eclipse centers. All results conditional on supplied cutout and local-baseline selections.
