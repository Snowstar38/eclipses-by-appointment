# Helper audit: T073-T080

One representative cutout per target was independently extracted from MAST TESSCut; helper_fetch.py records URL, member and hashes. helper_pixels.py makes helper-pixel-audit.json and each helper-difference.png. A median out-of-event minus in-event difference image peaks in the target's nearest or adjacent pixel in all eight, and each independent 3x3 target aperture shows a deep event. helper-contact.png is the visual contact sheet. T080 sector 16 shows an event truncated by a gap; do not use it as a complete timing. These are alternate reductions of the same photons, not new observing occasions.

Sky subtraction uses the median of positive, finite pixels with positive time-median and distance >3 pixels from target; 49-55 pixels are valid for these examples. Target flux and corrected baseline are positive. Neighbor test uses shared Gaia sources within 120 arcsec and compares their approximate G-band flux relative to target against the observed 3x3 fractional loss. No catalogued neighbor with a G magnitude exceeds that conservative bound. This neglects bandpass differences, aperture response, and unresolved or missing sources, so source localization is not absolute.

Gaia geometry convention check: the official DR3 model table documents SB2 order P, systemic velocity, K1, K2, e, argument, periastron time; SB1 omits K2. This matches lead_gaia_covariance.py's selected indices. The epoch is relative to Gaia reference epoch, and testing both conjunctions avoids the 180-degree ambiguity in choosing a component. Source: https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Gaia_archive/chap_datamodel/sec_dm_non--single_stars_tables/ssec_dm_nss_two_body_orbit.html . Gaia covariance is linear and formal; possible systematics or alias ambiguities are not included. Measured period discrepancies cannot be explained by a mere omega or epoch convention. Error in Gaia's orbit, an unresolved additional system, and mistaken cycle count are possible explanations, not established conclusions.

For T077, 14.291 d is a recurrence of the deep events, while twice this is near but differs from Gaia's 28.557 d SB2 period. At BTJD 2469.622 the nearest nominal Gaia conjunction is 2474.390, differing by 4.768 d versus a 0.279 d propagated linear formal uncertainty. These tensions leave association and orbital geometry unresolved; centroid agreement alone cannot distinguish unresolved stars. Independent spectroscopy and resolved imaging would help.
