# Independent helper audit

All three cases were inspected in actual P/P2 folded windows and full-sector context. Tesscut cutouts are same-photon alternative reductions, not independent observing occasions. The recorded WCS is ICRS target at Gaia coordinates (epoch motion not propagated); target positions and quality-zero time limits are in helper-pixel-summary.json. Helper plots sum target-centered circular apertures of radius 1.5 and 2.5 pixels, subtracting per-cadence median of pixels at radius >4 times aperture pixel count. Outer annulus is a diagnostic with the same subtraction; it is noisy and not an independent comparison star. Tesscut FLUX_BKG was also subtracted; instrumental variations remain. Vertical ranges clipped to 0.95–1.05. Positive gap/ramp context, not pipeline suppression alone, motivates interpretations below.

* H072: Fold P at 3021.668 is missing data around -0.5 to -0.25 d, so its negative core statistic is not a fully covered sensitive null. The 4119.3–4119.8 broad depression is seen in the smaller target-centered aperture and near a gap; a single insecure event remains plausible. Center, period, and source attribution are not established. Target WCS (5.61,5.60); Gaia neighbor query failed (503); unresolved neighbors may contribute.
* H073: s46 partial feature at 2567 begins after the orbit gap and resembles the ramp after the previous gap near 2553; this is positive systematic context for that selected signal. s72 trough near 3270 is embedded in multiday varying baseline, steps at ~3266.5 and ~3273.2, not a clean separated event. These are not demonstrated same-kind repeats. Target WCS s46 (5.59,5.01), s72 (5.68,5.30); neighbor cone unavailable.
* H077: s104 low plateaus 4180–85 and 4193–98 with abrupt changes around 4185 and 4198, rather than a securely isolated eclipse. Smaller aperture reproduces the pattern. Sectors 105–107 cutouts were retrieved and visually inspected; no compelling obvious comparable isolated event in these plots. They are exploratory, not validated sensitive nulls. Actual time endpoints and maximum internal gaps are recorded; raw orbit ramps and background are significant. Target WCS s104 (5.67,5.97); cone unavailable.
