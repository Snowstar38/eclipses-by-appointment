New aperture 1/3/5 depths 12.90/11.41/10.58 ppt show modest aperture dilution.

Recomputing the old pair phase with the measured period gives 0.747038 against Gaia 0.7735 +/-0.0079, retaining about 3.35-sigma phase tension. The historical grazing model (e about 0.411, omega 344.5 deg, R1 about 1.76 solar radii, R2 about 0.52, m2 about 0.5) fits both eclipse shapes and stellar scales; the 0.026% period change alters Keplerian scales by less than 0.02%. This is a physically consistent eclipse model with a discrepant Gaia eccentricity combination, not evidence that Gaia errors are known to be wrong. No arbitrary sigma threshold is used as a veto.
