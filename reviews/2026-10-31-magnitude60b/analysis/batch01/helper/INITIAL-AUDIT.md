# Independent helper audit (initial; final numeric freeze pending)

Reviewed the ten historical difference-image PNGs and corresponding JSON and Gaia NSS files. `attribution-audit.json` contains exact data, input paths and SHA256 hashes, and `audit_extract.py` reproduces the extraction. The image windows were predicted from the search ephemeris, not measured eclipse times; centroid offsets are point estimates, not confidence regions. Gaia G based brightness capability is approximate and does not model TESS band or PRF. A neighbor intrinsic eclipse requirement is not comparable directly to a blended-pixel fractional loss. Custom TESScut and QLP reductions share photons.

- TIC 336856559: strongly crowded. Sector 103 eight-window stack puts the loss 0.211 pixel from target and 2.26 pixels from the only brightness-capable Gaia neighbor (G 9.994, 45.9 arcsec). Image loss peaks at target while the out-of-eclipse light peaks near the bright neighbor. Fractional depths 99.7, 54.2, 27.3 ppt in 1/3/5-pixel apertures demonstrate strong aperture dependence consistent with dilution; this cannot establish exactly why historical QLP depth is about 141 ppt in another sector. PSF fractional residual 0.94 is poor, so no precision source assignment from that fit.
- TIC 42960522: centroid 0.307 pixel from target; closest capable neighbors are 1.55 and 1.61 pixels from deficit. Loss visually centered near target; crowded-field and centroid limitations remain.
- TIC 374589618: centroid 0.099 pixel from target and 0.67 pixel from closest capable neighbor, G 17.28 at 13.5 arcsec (approximate intrinsic eclipse needed 79%). Shallow image is noisy; source assignment is supported but not geometrically conclusive. Additional pixel work would strengthen it.
- TIC 146878534, 123220126, 355885228, 169303527, 94541119, 409935170, and 365864542: deficit is within 0.24 pixel of target, and no Gaia neighbor within 60 arcsec passes this rough brightness capability threshold. Images visually favor target. The approximate threshold does not establish absence of unresolved sources.

Historical SAP event depths independently support alternating branches. Search spacing for 123220126, 374589618, 355885228, 409935170 is half their full NSS periods (7.9648895, 3.1662004, 12.2241552, 8.7056187 days respectively). Even/odd median depths are 126.03/85.30, 4.45/7.72, 24.74/14.28, 118.18/115.29 ppt. For 409935170 the aggregate median conceals a clearer alternating difference in sector 13 and sector dependence; parity selection requires checking actual fitted morphology. The 374589618 shallow signal is noisy and warrants corroboration before treating the depth difference as precise. Parities are rounded from historical event coordinates and are not measured times; the full-period same-kind fitted timing belongs in lead's final results.

All numerical fits, derived geometries, reports and actual hash manifest remain to be checked at final freeze.
