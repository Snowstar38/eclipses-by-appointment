# Prospective triage comparison

The fixed cohort is the 402 remaining Fable candidates at public revision 3ae245b41eda9e8b24cc4f93611704ce879daba1. The primary question is which ordering would have delivered more actual catalogue additions for the same number of inspected apertures. The preceding 45 individually selected cases motivated the original queue priorities and are excluded from this evaluation. Each trial unit is an original TIC aperture. Attribute an accepted neighbouring host to the aperture where the review recovered it. Give a unique new host a single credit if two apertures lead to it, breaking ties by smallest numeric original TIC. Document such ties. The 707 lower-tier candidates were selected for having at least some eclipse evidence, and these 402 are the remainder after previous screens. Results describe this particular remaining queue.

## Inputs fixed before the new outcomes

Use only the November 22 candidate tables and their original saved measurements, plus the published November 24 positional/period flags. No pair verdicts, new measurements, new literature results, new images or current adjudications enter any rank. Save all 402 rows, the input hashes and the hash of each original target measurement. Three fixed orderings are compared.

1. **Fable rank** sorts the original numerical rank score high first, breaking ties by numerical TIC.
2. **Existing priority** uses the actual queue order from batches.csv. The original saved-photometry cut and archive comparison were used ahead of Fable rank.
3. **Source and clock** sorts first by the sum of four binary indicators, then by Fable rank, then numerical TIC. Each contributes one point: the blind period search agreed with the original clock, at least four measured events cleared Fable’s significance threshold and comprise at least half of the measurable events, the profile duty fraction was below 0.12 and either the secondary had signal-to-noise of at least five or odd/even depths differed by at least five sigma, and no flux-capable neighbour was recorded within 42 arcseconds. Missing evidence receives no point. These four indicators are an initial hypothesis, fixed without fitting to this review's outcomes.

The main review budgets are 23 and 100 of 402. Report cumulative additions found, fraction of reviewed apertures added, and recall of total additions if nonzero. The expected count for a uniformly random order is budget times total additions divided by 402. Full ordering curves are descriptive. Resolve signal even if a later check finds a known eclipse, and keep original versus reassigned host when available.

## Endpoints and uncertainty

The primary endpoint is inclusion as confirmed or probable in the final dated catalogue release. All reviewed apertures go in the denominator for operational yield. A held aperture counts as a review without a catalogue addition, with its unresolved status recorded separately. It cannot be interpreted as a non-eclipse. Repeat with confirmed additions alone. Report published/known physically attributed eclipses separately and, where the adjudication supplies a secure identification, also tabulate the broader endpoint of an identified eclipse source (added or known). A catalogue coincidence or an unresolved offset alone is insufficient. Report unresolved, directly refuted/non-eclipse, other known source and missing data separately where the final evidence permits. Report which rows received follow-up beyond the initial paired screen. The review order and selective follow-up can influence the chance that a source is proved. No extrapolation to all TESS stars or perfect ground truth is justified.

Report cohort sizes, positive and unresolved counts under each of the four indicators individually and under the saved-photometry cut, original bright-neighbour flag and lane. Add Wilson 95% intervals for observed yield as descriptive uncertainty for comparable future selected candidates. Bounds obtained by treating all unresolved cases as failures versus possible eventual successes are identification bounds, not confidence intervals. Compare original and new strategies separately within the two-minute and full-frame strata at the same relative fraction of each stratum where data permit. No tuning of thresholds, training on the 402, claims of calibrated probabilities, p-value selection or validation on the earlier 45. Report low event counts and uncertainty for all conclusions.
