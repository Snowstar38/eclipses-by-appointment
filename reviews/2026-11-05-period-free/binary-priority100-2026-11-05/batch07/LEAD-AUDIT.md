# Batch07 lead audit - T061-T070

All ten fixed queue identities were reviewed without substitution. The source packet and original bulk result for each TIC, every used cached QLP FITS, the shared Gaia NSS and 120-arcsec neighbor catalogues, and the queue are covered by `SOURCE-HASHES.json`. `lead_measure.py` generated representative raw SAP views and exploratory local fits; `lead_sector_plots.py` displayed all cached sectors with raw and KSPSAP flux. I inspected the actual plotted shapes. `lead_fold_coverage.py` samples Gaia-period point predictions and records core endpoints and time-bin coverage; it does not establish a sensitive null across the propagated timing allowance. `lead_gap_evidence.py` records nearby gaps and steps. `lead_gaia_geometry.py` and `lead_gaia_covariance.py` assess edge-on conjunctions using each source's Gaia solution and covariance. The current TESSCut footprint was checked for each target. New sectors 109 for T064 and 107/108 for T070 were fetched and inspected with WCS target positions and uncorrected pixel sums. New-sector receipt hashes are preserved.

The final physical outcomes are two REFUTED flags (T061 and T068), two additional strongly shaped false flags (T064 and T066), and six PLAUSIBLE interpretations (T062, T063, T065, T067, T069, T070). No candidate meets CONFIRMED or PROBABLE criteria. This is a priority-selected batch, not an occurrence or search-yield estimate. All ten novelty fields are UNKNOWN because the shared queue catalogue work leaves four VizieR gates unavailable; no query was repeated here. Period, period error and primary epoch fields are blank because no measured unique eclipse ephemeris survives.

T061 is the clearest positive refutation. Its two huge s84 QLP SAP troughs are contradicted by a valid active-sky-corrected TESSCut aperture containing the target and bright 23.72-arcsec neighbor: deficits of 0.042% and 0.408% versus QLP 15.07% and 24.11%. The raw sky brightens by 50.6 and 88.6 counts per pixel at the putative events. The 11x11 cutout has 44 zero edge pixels, so a lowest-pixel background estimator would be invalid; the helper used active-sky pixels and preserved the cutout and scripts. The small residuals cannot be assigned to either star because their point-spread functions are edge-truncated. Terra's independent `174306672/helper-audit.txt` records the deciding pixel evidence.

T068's retained dip is a raw brightening/ramp, about +12 ppt in the core, and is REFUTED as an eclipse. T064 s88's shallow valley lies between large brightenings and a gap/step; newly available s109 target-containing pixels show another pair of huge brightenings and a quiet conditional Gaia phase. T066 s86's selected dip is an unobserved ingress/low branch before a 0.53 d gap, followed by a long rise, while the nearest Gaia conjunction differs by 9.98 d against a 1.71 d linear allowance. Those positive raw shapes support REFUTED verdicts for the selected flags, without claiming the stars cannot be binaries.

T062, T063, T065 and T067 each have an insecure possible single raw depression. Sloping baselines, gaps, low amplitude and suppression by KSPSAP make artifacts plausible, but do not positively refute the single-event interpretations. They are retained PLAUSIBLE. Conditional Gaia-period point checks are used as context, not calibrated sensitive nulls or a substitute for a measured period.

T069 has broad ~4-8 ppt aperture depressions at three Gaia-phase occasions, reproduced by Terra in target-containing active-sky-corrected 3x3 TESSCut sums from s25, s26 and s52. The s52 phase trough is one of several quasi-periodic sector oscillations; morphology, timing and source attribution are not secure. The same-photon alternative reduction supports aperture variation, not an independent observing occasion or eclipse classification. Terra's `159851783/helper-audit.txt` documents the extraction. T070's s6 shallow notch is unconfirmed; new s108 has a WCS target pixel median of only ~0.15 counts, making its apparent Gaia-phase window an invalid target-flux null. T070 remains PLAUSIBLE.

`RESULTS.csv` separates completion, physical verdict and novelty. Every report names the limitation and next check. The lead and helper scripts and numeric JSON files reproduce the material measurements; `SHA256SUMS.json` freezes all batch outputs after the final report generation.
