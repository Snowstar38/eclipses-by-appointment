# Batch01 lead audit

The exact sealed H001–H010 queue was reviewed without substitution. All ten are `cross_method_dip` selections. I inspected quality-zero raw SAP plots of representative retained windows and full cached-sector context, using the frozen 66 FITS files. `inspect_raw.py` checked every FITS against its original bulk-result SHA-256 before making panels. The independent helper checked the same 66 receipts, inspected all flags and made separate TESSCut/WCS reductions for the two strong cases. `helper-audit.md` records their exceptions and limits.

Verdicts: **1 confirmed (H002), 3 plausible (H005, H006, H008), 6 refuted selected eclipse interpretations (H001, H003, H004, H007, H009, H010)**. Each bounded review is complete. A refutation here applies to the selected signal/window and does not assert that a hidden eclipse anywhere in the star's history is impossible. No target is counted as a new system yet: the central catalogue worker marks all novelty `UNKNOWN` because remote scoped gates could not be completed.

H002 has two complete flat-bottom raw dips in different sectors; local fits give BTJD 2040.0819 and 4106.0797 and a conditional N35 period 59.0285 d. Two partial occasions reinforce the clock without precision centers: s94 shows a low state and egress near 3871.3, about half an eclipse duration after the N35 predicted center 3869.966; s103 shows ingress near 4163.8 and egress near 4166.3 around predicted 4165.108, with a central gap. N34 has a clean s13 null and N36 has a largely quiet s94 predicted window near 3876.52; s103 alternative windows are contaminated by the actual event and are not clean nulls. N35 agrees with Gaia within about 1.2 formal period errors and with Gaia B conjunction to 0.0007 phase. These are bounded nearby-cycle tests, not an exhaustive exclusion of unrelated long-period aliases. The reported period is rounded to 59.03 d and has no claimed calibrated error. Difference flux, neighbor bounds, and independent aperture support the target, with the same-photon and bandpass limitations stated in the report.

H008 has a complete s70 event and a partly observed s92 decline/low state. An 18-cycle period is compatible with Gaia, but all checked N15–N21 alternatives lack an independent sensitive null and the s92 egress is quality censored; the result period is left blank. The target difference image supports the source despite one distant flux-capable neighbor. This is a completed plausible review, not unperformed work coded as uncertainty.

H006's narrow s76 trough is not reproduced at the assumed Gaia phase in clean s75 and s81 windows. That tests the Gaia-period interpretation only; the isolated feature remains physically ambiguous. H005 has one insecure shallow near-gap dip, and its s95 SAP has pathological near-zero samples. A current TESScut sector-inventory query for H005/H006/H008 returned HTTP 200 and exactly the cached sector sets, with no additional sector to test; receipts and response hashes are in the batch. Attribution checks were concentrated on H002/H008, where real eclipse interpretation could be decided.

Files: each TIC folder has `REPORT.md`, `raw-inspection.json`, `raw-windows.png`, `orbit-context.json/png`; the deciding cases also have local fit, coverage and alias artifacts. `inspect_raw.py`, `orbit_context.py`, `refine_events.py`, `check_cycles.py`, `test_aliases.py`, and `check_single.py` reproduce the lead numerics and plots. `helper-hashes.json` and `SHA256SUMS.json` seal the local outputs; original FITS and bulk JSON hashes are stored in per-target raw artifacts and shared source packets.
