# Batch 10 lead audit

All ten frozen H091–H100 `period_fold_near` rows were retained. `inspect_fold.py` checked the frozen bulk and fold receipt hashes and every referenced QLP FITS hash, read all available sectors of raw SAP flux, recorded actual cadence endpoints and gaps, and measured both full-period and half-period predicted windows with separate core and flank counts. Every full sector and both folds were plotted. The root raw preview was not used for verdicts because its single-event selection may be unrelated to the fold. `raw-fold.json` preserves each sector's SHA256, actual window coverage, local contrast and available alternate columns.

The selected fold has positive contrary evidence in H091, H092, H093, H095, H096, H097, H098 and H099: broad sector trends, post-gap recoveries or level changes supply the apparent contrast, while other adequately sampled predicted cores are flat or opposite in sign. Their `REFUTED` verdicts concern the selected repeating eclipse claim, not every possible binary configuration. Quality-zero counts were never treated as coverage by themselves; the reports give core and both flanks, while the numeric file gives sector endpoints and internal gaps. Predicted times are not event centers.

H094 has shallow depressions near the predicted windows in sectors 33 and 87, both around 1.4 ppt. Sector 33 survives KSPSAP; sector 6 lacks a right baseline and is not a sensitive null. New sector 108 has actual target placement (WCS pixel 2.79, 2.26 and 6815 e/s central 3x3 flux) and a flat nominal screening window with 52 core and 129/130 flank cadences; its 1.2 ppt single-cadence scatter and uncalibrated time correlation limit exclusion. Sector 98 has only about 7 e/s in the nominal aperture and is unusable. Their weak shape, attribution and cycle aliases prevent a measured photometric period. H100 has an isolated sector-86 dip near 3640.16 at about 6.8 ppt, with a synchronized SAP_X excursion; sector 58 is a genuine observed null for the exact screened period. The pixel difference is dominated by a broad background change, without secure localization. Fresh s59/s85 cutouts contain no nominal predicted core (their time spans end before the relevant centers), and their nominal aperture flux is near zero, so neither tests recurrence. The centroid coincidence raises an aperture/neighbor alternative but cannot alone prove an instrumental artifact. Both are retained `PLAUSIBLE`. No Gaia spectroscopic period or generic fold period has been copied to a measured eclipse-period field.

Novelty is `UNKNOWN` for all ten, following the central catalogue audit; cached primary nonmatches do not clear the four unavailable VizieR gates. The Terra helper independently audited deciding raw windows and alternate flux/centroid columns in helper-prefixed artifacts. Further checks of newer sectors for H094 and pixels for H100 are recorded in the helper audit as available. Same-photon alternate reductions were used only as processing checks, not repeat observing occasions.

The bounded outcomes do not support survey completeness or a false-alarm probability. The source packet, QLP FITS, bulk and fold receipts are captured in `SOURCE-HASHES.json`; `SHA256SUMS.json` seals the deliverables.
