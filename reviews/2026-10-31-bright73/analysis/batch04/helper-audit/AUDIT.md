# Independent helper audit

Read the campaign brief, delegation, privacy policy and current paper section 2.7. Inspected the ten-target raw-event contact sheet, saved timing/alias data, geometry and target-neighbour difference-image measurements; checked the final result fields against measurements and inspected the deciding exception reports. This is a bounded audit, not a new all-sector fit or an independent observation.

The separate weighted least-squares calculation in `check.py` reproduces all ten saved periods, epochs and scatter-scaled errors to numerical precision, using measured fitted midpoints rather than predicted windows. `timing-check.json` records the calculation. For TIC 239572145 stable alternating ~150/55 ppt events require the doubled period; the final fit uses six deeper same-kind events and excludes the singular S82 center. Its period 6.3129271986 d and epoch BTJD 1714.9544368 reproduce independently. The old half-period alias description was corrected by the lead. Other aliases have within-sector consecutive recurrences in the saved audit, though no exhaustive alias scan is claimed.

TIC 382631802 is appropriately retained PROBABLE with review COMPLETE: shallow dips and timing ride variable structure, timing reduced chi-square ~126 is inflated, and the approximate central duration 3.21 h versus fitted median 7.58 h is unresolved, not proof of impossibility under uncertain stellar and shape assumptions. The S97 target deficit centroid is 0.220 pixels from target versus about 2.38 and 2.67 pixels from the two capable Gaia neighbours; the source map's invalid edge pixels require the explicitly recorded central-box peak. Residual 0.46 and baseline variability limit localization precision. TIC 134193638 has strong oscillations and chi-square ~9; the report retains shape and timing-systematic limitations.

For TIC 239572145 a capable neighbour is 28.3 arcsec away; the deficit centroid is 0.068 pixel from the target and 1.411 pixels from that neighbour. The historical stack mixes eclipse kinds and is identified as such. TIC 113810700 has a capable neighbour 57.1 arcsec away, with centroid distances 0.097 and 2.846 pixels from target and neighbour. The required intrinsic neighbour eclipse is not compared directly with the blended pixel fractional loss. Other saved maps and the target identities were checked in the batch pixel audit. Alternative TESScut reductions share the original photons and do not constitute independent observing occasions.

Geometry offsets and propagated uncertainties remain descriptive: in particular TIC 239572145 +0.1907 d versus 0.0717 d linear Gaia uncertainty and TIC 90285860 -0.4519 d versus 0.2020 d are recorded in reports; no arbitrary sigma rejection is imposed. Unavailable or flagged windows are not clean nulls. All targets have multiple measured raw events, so no apparent single-cycle case requires additional current-sector availability investigation.

Final RESULTS has exactly the ten queue identities, ten extant reports, and all four numerical ephemeris/depth fields exactly match target measurement files. It records nine confirmed and one probable, all complete. Formal timing errors retain the stated fit/scatter meaning and exclude uncalibrated systematics. No public or canonical file was changed.
