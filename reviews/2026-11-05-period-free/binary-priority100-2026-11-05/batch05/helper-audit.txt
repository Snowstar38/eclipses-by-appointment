T042 (TIC 255912894): Independent sector 66 TESSCut pixel extraction, using 47 core and 190 side quality-zero samples, yields a 3.51 ppt dip in the raw target-centered 3x3 aperture; outer-pixel background subtraction gives 3.62 ppt. The side-minus-core difference map peaks at the target location (5.43, 6.00). The similarly bright Gaia source at (8.54, 4.13) shows a negative, not positive, difference; another bright source at (1.58, 5.33) shows no clear positive peak. This favors the target vicinity over those resolved bright stars. Attribution remains unresolved for closer capable neighbors, particularly G=16.19 at (5.60, 6.80) and G=14.43 at (5.26, 7.90); approximate Gaia G flux ratios are 0.0105 and 0.0535 relative to target, with bandpass and aperture differences. The outer-pixel sample is fully finite and has no zero pixels, so the zero-pixel failure does not apply; field stars in it may still influence background. Extraction uses the same photons as QLP and is not an independent observing occasion. Difference image is descriptive, not a fitted localization or a proof excluding close neighbors.
