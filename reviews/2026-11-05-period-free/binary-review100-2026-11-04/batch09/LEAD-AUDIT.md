# Batch 09 lead audit

All ten frozen H081–H090 brightening leads were retained. Source packet, bulk and fold hashes and every referenced QLP FITS hash were rechecked. All cached sectors were plotted as raw SAP with QUALITY=0, along with every flagged positive window. The numeric artifact for each target records real BTJD endpoints, internal gaps, core and flank counts, pre/post changes, available alternate columns, and diagnostic period peaks. No screening center was used as a fitted event time.

The clearest non-eclipse variability is H085: a 3.050–3.064 d sawtooth in four sectors. H083 has broad repeated waves with 5.8–6.5 d and harmonic diagnostic peaks. H081 is a coherent short-sector wave with later raw scales problematic. H087 has an impulsive rise and decaying tail, a flare candidate. None is promoted to an eclipsing binary or assigned the Gaia spectroscopic period as a photometric measurement.

H086 is a few-cadence flare-shaped excursion coincident with a large background ramp and Y-centroid shift. The independent helper correctly challenged an artifact verdict based on those correlations alone. A newly fetched sector-32 11×11 TESSCut difference image places the positive excess well below the WCS target: nearest-pixel-centered target 3×3 gains only +1.7 counts while the exterior of target 5×5 gains +94.7 counts. That positive off-target image evidence supports REFUTED for the selected target brightening. It does not disprove a separate weaker binary signal. H088's positive mound intersects a 1.005 d data gap and a very large local background change; neither proves an artifact, so H088 remains UNRESOLVED. H082, H084, H089 and H090 also contain positive apertural excursions with insecure source and/or instrumental origin. H084's slope-selected sector-5 feature is false, but a distinct sector-98 transient prevents treating the whole target as settled by that window alone. Its cutout difference has a positive/negative dipole around the WCS target, leaving stellar versus instrumental cause unresolved.

No target has a measured eclipse depth, center, or binary period. The 0.5–15 d Lomb–Scargle peaks in `period-scan.json` are diagnostics of the selected raw light curves; sector trends and harmonics can affect them. Corrected-flux suppression and centroid changes were not treated as source proof. TESSCut checks for H084/H086/H087 use the same FFI exposures and unregistered local differences; `pixel-difference.json` records exact WCS, masks and frames. H087's positive image moment is at (5.80,4.99), near target WCS (5.78,5.09), with +452.9 counts in target 3×3. This supports a targetlike flare candidate but cannot resolve subpixel blends or prove flare physics. The independent `helper-audit.md` and `helper-cadences.json` challenged H086/H088 artifact certainty; final verdicts incorporate that audit and the subsequent pixel check. The central catalogue file leaves novelty UNKNOWN for every target because four gates are unavailable; cached primary nonmatch was not counted as novelty clearance.
