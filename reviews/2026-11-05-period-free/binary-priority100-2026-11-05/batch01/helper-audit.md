# Helper audit: batch01

Independent audit of decisive windows, Gaia neighbors, pixel extractions, orbit geometry and selected aliases. All findings are bounded to the checked data, not a new observing occasion when the same TESS exposures are re-extracted.

- T001 S22 difference centroid 0.077 pixel from target; 118.97 ppt 5x5 TESSCut vs 137.56 ppt QLP SAP. Gaia DR3 1629287391244313856 is 2.71 arcsec away, G flux ratio 0.414, requiring an ideal 47% disappearance to explain observed depth. TESS cannot resolve this neighbor; source ownership remains open.
- T002 S4 difference centroid 0.206 pixel, independent extraction 109.23 ppt vs 110.86 SAP. No G-capable source within 120 arcsec. Measured two-event separation approximately 714.0049 d admits 28, 29, 30 cycles at 25.5002, 24.6209, 23.8002 d; no extra well-covered predictions in cached sectors for these choices. Gaia agreement does not select a cycle uniquely.
- T003 S97 and T004 S65 difference centroids 0.122 and 0.084 pixel; alternate pixel extraction 159.09 and 105.46 ppt versus SAP 139.67 and 126.67. No G-capable neighbor within 120 arcsec in the ideal disappearance bound.
- T005 S10: 23.82 ppt in 5x5 TESSCut versus 57.02 SAP, centroid 0.078 pixel from target, closest capable neighbor 1.91 pixel from difference centroid. Corrected QLP KSPSAP suppresses the event; this is not artifact proof, and pixel extraction supports it.
- T006 S32: 32.85 ppt TESSCut, 31.37 ppt SAP, 30.01/31.37/32.18 ppt QLP corrected apertures. Difference centroid 0.165 pixel; only capable Gaia neighbor is 119 arcsec away. Two-event separation 43.062 d: midpoint 2204.770 has full core/side coverage and 0.17 ppt deficit, contradicting the 21.531 d two-cycle alias; 14.354 and 10.7655 d aliases also encounter actual sensitive windows. Conditional on same-kind events and fixed period.

Nearby neighbors and baseline variation remain concerns.
- T008 S62 event ingress is visible before a ~1.6 d gap followed by depressed flux and recovery; fit at 3002.404 is gap edge, not a measured center. S62 TESSCut has much of footprint zero and target at detector edge, so its sign conflict is not a reliable null. S109 target aperture baseline 6653, quality-0 BTJD 4318.5608–4338.7357, and 1st/99th percentiles 0.99365/1.00473 of median; no deep event in this interval, but this alone does not resolve arbitrary periods.
- T009 S100 difference centroid 0.321 pixel from target and at least 1.86 pixel from an ideal-capable neighbor; TESSCut 10.36 ppt vs SAP 13.92. These are approximate positional supports, not calibrated exclusion radii.
- T010 S88 target-centered 3x3 TESSCut directly contradicts QLP: simultaneous 103 core cadences yield 5558.39 vs side medians 5550.36, 5554.19 (−1.10 ppt), while QLP 0.96695 vs 0.99775, 0.99841 gives 31.19 ppt. WCS target (5.646,5.472); target-aperture baseline 5552 shows nonzero sensitivity. Plot helper-T010-comparison.png. Physical origin of QLP rectangle is not established. S109 cutout near-zero target flux, not a sensitive null.
