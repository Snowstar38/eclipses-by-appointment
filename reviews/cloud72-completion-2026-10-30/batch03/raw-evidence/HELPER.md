The local period fit assumes search cycle numbering; aliases remain possible. Depths vary with aperture, blending and event fit. Signed difference images use medians of pre/post and in-event cadences; the custom extraction and model comparisons are in pixel_independent.py, plot_pixels.py, source_gaussian.py and fixed_source_comparison.py. Neighbours are TIC 60-arcsec cone sources with Tmag capable of an ideal total eclipse given observed 3x3 depth; the estimate ignores aperture throughput and colour, catalogue incompleteness and unknown close companions. Fixed coordinate Gaussian SSE ratios and free-centroid formal errors are diagnostic, not calibrated PRF likelihoods, and do not include systematics. Centroids from clipped 5x5 pixels are biased and not used alone for source attribution. tesscut-downloads.json supplies URLs, zip hashes, and pixel-results.json FITS hashes and event coverage. TIC cone query receipts are stored per target. The lead decides geometry and final verdicts.
