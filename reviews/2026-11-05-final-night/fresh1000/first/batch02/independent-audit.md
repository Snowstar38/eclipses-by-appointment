# Independent audit — Z010–Z016

I inspected the six lead raw-event plots, all seven custom TESScut localization plots, and the second Z011 sector-33 image. Quality-zero cutout reductions use the same TESS photons as the lead light curves; they are not additional observations. The scripts, FITS cutouts, response CSVs and JSON measurements are retained in this batch; helper-numeric-audit.json records SHA-256 hashes of each helper evidence file. Current TESScut sector inventory, query timestamps, URLs and response hashes are in lead-current-sector-inventory.json (produced by helper_inventory.py). S21 is the only listed sector for Z016. ESA Gaia TAP returned HTTP 503 twice; Heidelberg Gaia DR3 TAP mirror succeeded and responses and hashes are saved.

The Gaia DR3 60 arcsec cones are bounded, not exhaustive. Neighbor capacity is estimated as measured 3x3 blended loss multiplied by (1+r)/r for the neighbor/target Gaia G flux ratio r. This assumes complete capture of only those two sources; Gaia G differs from TESS band, aperture throughput and other sources matter. It is a rough plausibility indicator, not a calibrated exclusion or comparison of intrinsic neighbor loss directly with blended loss. Positive-difference centroids restricted to 3 pixels are not PRF fits; unlisted close blends remain possible.

- Z010 TIC 150606032: S7 shallow bounded event in raw and cutout. Lead's added multiple occasions imply ~18.685 d bycatch, rather than the Gaia 27.426 d candidate; timing scatter and aliases remain. 3x3 loss 0.00337, positive centroid offset 0.561 pixel, and several potentially capable neighbors, including Gaia 5594800841328741120 at 27.20 arcsec (G 12.980) and 5594801013129324288 at 11.74 arcsec (G 17.056); host uncertain. Probable aperture signal, not secure Gaia association.
- Z011 TIC 157000271: S7 repeated dips, with S33/S34 repeats measured by lead at a ~17.63 d clock, not Gaia 26.845 d. Independent S7 cutout shows deficit dominated by bright source north of target; S33 image shows same off-target source, while target-centered 3x3 aperture has no discernible event. Positive off-target evidence refutes the selected-target attribution; no specific contaminant identity asserted. Separate occasions, unlike the reduction of a single occasion, are genuinely separate observations.
- Z012 TIC 192831602: four clear narrow dips in S5/6/32/87; approximate ~39.17 d deep-event clock and Gaia period 39.1443 d are compatible within lead's Gaia covariance check. S87 difference image is near target (offset 0.192 pixel), but Gaia 4809169031311345920 at 21.07 arcsec and G 15.710 has idealized required intrinsic eclipse 0.338 for observed 0.00922 blended loss. No calibrated deblend; plausible alternative host, so probable with hostuncertain.
- Z013 TIC 198590540: repeated deep raw events are real, but S80 cutout difference peaks on a separate bright source ~2.46 pixels from selected target. Positive off-target evidence refutes attribution to selected target; ~8.93 d aperture clock is bycatch, not the Gaia 34.825 d orbit. No contaminant identity asserted.
- Z014 TIC 218292999: deep repeated ~37.92 d events and a distinct shallower event, not two same-kind cycles; Gaia period 37.9794 d is close but Gaia phase discrepancies must be reported with lead's covariance. S70 loss 0.072, centroid 0.054 pixel from target. Gaia 33692201666680832 at 28.3 arcsec has rough required intrinsic loss 1.72; because bandpass, throughput and other flux are unmodeled, this is not a rigorous exclusion. Probable, hostuncertain; geometry conditional on source ownership and event-kind interpretation.
- Z015 TIC 233624506: repeated deep and shallower different-kind events support ~29.006 d and lead reports Gaia phase consistency for both kinds. S81 loss 0.107, centroid offset 0.035 pixel. No catalogued neighbor within 60 arcsec has idealized required intrinsic loss below 2; 2.4 arcsec neighbor requires ~81.75. Target attribution is strongly favored; C is reasonable under ordinary unresolved-companion and bandpass caveats. Geometry is conditional on source association and event-kind interpretation.
- Z016 TIC 16150832: exploratory S21 center BTJD 1896.9136 and duration 0.48 d, not a fitted ephemeris. The 3x3 cutout shows a ~0.003 loss and target-centered deficit, but sparse sampling, trend and gaps limit morphology. Gaia cone has no neighbor requiring <2 under approximation or within 5 arcsec. Only S21 is presently listed in TESScut inventory; no repeated event or Gaia-period confirmation. Plausible, not confirmed.

Lead owns final scientific verdicts, timing/geometry and numerical freeze; their per-target reports retain assumptions and limitations. I support P/P/C for Z012/14/15 respectively, P hostuncertain for Z010, selected-target refutations for Z011/13, and plausible Z016.
