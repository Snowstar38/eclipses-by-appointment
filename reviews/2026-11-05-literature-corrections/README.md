# Prior-literature corrections

**R706**. Prior-known KIC 2697935 / KOI-3853: Kepler Eclipsing Binary Catalog, Beck et al. (2014), P=21.5133595 d. The catalogue ephemeris matches the TESS events. Physical probable tier and unresolved source-attribution caveat retained; excluded from new-system claims.

**R725**. Prior-known TIC 219345200 / Gaia DR3 4785405080341786368: Zwintz et al. (2026) explicitly identify an eclipsing binary, citing Beck et al. (2024); also TOI-706. The published 65-day period differs from our approximate 59.919-day recurrence. That discrepancy and source ownership remain unresolved; physical probable tier retained, excluded from new-system claims.

**R727**. Prior-known TIC 238855958: Gill et al. (2020), MNRAS 491, 1548, an F-M eclipsing binary at 38.20 d. Physical confirmed tier retained; excluded from new-system claims.
