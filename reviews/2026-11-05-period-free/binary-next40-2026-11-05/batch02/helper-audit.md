F011: Historical vet20 REPORT section 2 and both saved difference images were inspected. The S21 and S48 difference flux peaks at the off-target source, with target-core fractional changes small in the prior analysis; the prior report attributes it to TIC 17238865, 60.4 arcsec away. Prior neighbor-aperture centers 1896.4278 and 2628.4835, depths 10.5% and 9.6%. The original TESScut files were in a temporary directory that is no longer present, so the saved prior measurement cannot be independently recomputed from pixels here. The source attribution is well-supported historical evidence, not a new measurement; QLP packet FITS are independent retained inputs. This says nothing about all possible eclipses of the target.

F014: Raw event panels show repeated similar eclipse-like dips in three sectors. The pairs 2424.6169/2431.2596, 2812.2604/2818.8631, 3340.8387/3347.4436 are real-looking individual events. Similarity does not by itself establish two eclipse kinds or orbital period. The known VSX EB 38.35 arcsec away has period 1.1016829 d and Gaia G=16.45928, versus target G=10.79955. The G-band flux ratio 0.00545 makes it unlikely to explain 17 to 24 ppt events even in a total eclipse, subject to bandpass and aperture differences; its VSX period also produces phase shifts of 0.135 and 0.343 cycles for the measured same-kind events between sectors. Other capable neighbors and actual WCS still require assessment. S15 is sparsely sampled. No geometry claim based solely on Gaia period.
