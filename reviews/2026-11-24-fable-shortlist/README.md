# Fable’s lower-tier TESS shortlist

Fable 5.2 left 707 measured TESS candidates below its original 700 probable candidates. By the start of this review, 258 of the 707 had a matching TIC in the project’s published decision or screening tables. We checked every candidate against earlier source catalogues and performed individual follow-up on 45 of the other 449. Three enter the physical catalogue, two confirmed and one probable. All three had public TESS threshold crossings before the project’s review. Fable 5.2 supplied the candidate list.

## What remains on the shortlist

[Current outcome for all 707 TICs](current-shortlist.csv) · [Original queue crosswalk](shortlist.csv) · [60 candidates selected using Fable’s saved measurements](photometry-priority.csv)

The current queue retains 402 targets for further work. Of these, 23 passed the saved-photometry cuts and had no position or period warning in the project, IJspeert and Pr?a comparison. They comprise 16 full-frame and seven two-minute targets. Additional checks of the published APOGEE table resolved two targets outside the 45 individually investigated fields.

The original list splits into 610 two-minute and 97 full-frame targets. After exact-TIC comparisons with the historical ledger, the scientific register and the dated field reviews, 355 and 94 respectively lacked a decision in the tables checked. The comparison uses TICs and coordinates because the Gaia identifiers in Fable’s original candidate CSVs lost precision. A historical screen may be a rejection or an unresolved source. Separate position and period comparisons identify different TICs belonging to the same field.

The saved-photometry selection required four significant primary events, a secondary at a signal-to-noise ratio of five or higher, a narrow eclipse profile and agreement with Fable’s blind period search. Sixty of the 449 met those cuts. Of those, 50 had no positional or matching-period flag in the local catalogue comparison, and 45 of those 50 had a neighbour bright enough to explain the measured fade. The 45 targets taken to individual follow-up were selected by their respective pipelines. They partially overlap the 60. The current outcome table distinguishes all three selection steps and retains withheld sources.

The [two-minute lane](twomin/README.md) re-extracted light curves for 20 targets and compared source positions for ten of those fields. The [full-frame lane](ffi/README.md) checked both phases in two sectors for 25 more targets. Three have previously reported eclipses in a neighbouring aperture. Twenty were left unresolved because of closer Gaia sources. The two remaining full-frame targets received the separate timing and source fits above. Earlier observers and their measured periods are identified in the [alternate-source record](previous-hosts/README.md).

## Previous identifications

All 707 input positions were compared with the IJspeert 2021 and 2024 eclipse tables, Prša’s 2022 TESS catalogue and the current project catalogue. We retained exact TICs, positions within 30 arcseconds, and sources within 15 arcminutes whose available period was within 0.2% of the tested period or its quarter, half, double or quadruple. The 2021 positional extract does not include periods. Period coverage in the project comparison is given below. The HEASARC distribution of VSX returned Algol in each of 45 separate query batches. For the 707 proposed positions we found zero exact TIC or 30-arcsecond matches among 798 distinct sources across 19 of Théo Chopard-Vilhem’s released and working tables. For the three adopted targets we also checked their exact Gaia DR3 identifiers. Reassigned positions were checked separately. [Credit check for each new host](added-host-credit.csv). [Position and period matches](catalogue-matches.csv) · [VSX matches within 54 arcseconds](vsx-matches.csv) · [VSX controls and queries](vsx-queries.json) · [Théo comparison](chopard-vilhem-matches.csv)

Kounkel et al. (2021) tabulated a TESS classification alongside their spectroscopy. Their Table 1 flags 369 of 8,105 rows as detached eclipsing binaries. We compared all 369 positions against this shortlist and against the 2,036 hosts in the catalogue at the start of the review. Three of the 707 were previously identified by Kounkel and colleagues, including TIC 9029822. Ten other stars in the previously accepted catalogue match within 0.15 arcseconds, with no additional matches out to 30 arcseconds. Nine have published TESS periods. A30 has a detached-eclipse flag and no published photometric period. The earlier classification applies to all ten, including cases whose period is refined here. Their earlier project measurements remain accessible under the original review identifiers. The ten corrections and three new hosts bring the accepted total from 2,036 to 2,029, comprising 1,487 confirmed and 542 probable systems.
