# Individual review evidence

70 previously unreviewed Fable 5 batch candidates received individual review: **64 physical confirmations and six plausible cases**. They do not overlap the paper's existing object table. Together with the returned Fable reviews, the paper's physical sample is **273 systems (261 confirmed and 12 probable)**; the six plausible cases from this review and one from the returned batch are excluded.

The [catalogue reconciliation](../novelty-reconciliation/README.md) establishes that 48 of these confirmations meet the original uncatalogued definition, raising the paper's scoped count to **247**. The remaining 16 confirmations await valid ASAS-SN coverage; recovered original vetted-table receipts are retained, and failed repeat requests are not negative matches. Historical report novelty labels are superseded by the reconciliation and current register. These are internal project verdicts, not external peer review or precise component characterization. Existing tested code and valid prior evidence were reused.

[Machine-readable register](../../reviewed-extension.csv) has review IDs R001-R070, physical verdicts, novelty limitations, report paths and deciding next tests. [Updated candidate table](../../batch-candidates.csv) records the 70 completed reviews and leaves 104 unreviewed candidates. Numerical screening columns in that table retain their original meaning; use the reports for fitted measurements.

Archived per-source metadata and limitations are preserved in the reports. Report hashes are in `MANIFEST.json`.
