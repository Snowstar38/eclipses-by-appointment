Exact frozen queue: ten distinct targets, unchanged `queue.csv`. `lead_source_hashes.py` verifies the ten source packets, ten bulk results and 88 QLP FITS against original SHA256 receipts; `LEAD-SOURCE-HASHES.json` records each file. `lead_measure.py` inspects quality-zero positive raw SAP, actual time endpoints and gaps for all flagged windows and produces per-target numeric measurements and visual event panels. The contact montage is `lead-raw-montage.jpg`. Local trapezoids are diagnostics; bound-hitting or singular fits do not furnish precise eclipse timing. `lead_alternative.py` checks QLP KSPSAP where available on the same photons. Many later sectors do not provide that column, and absence is recorded instead of calling it a null. No bulk score was turned directly into a verdict.

F032 has three resolved, narrow same-shape events in independent sectors S34, S61 and S88. Their consecutive 44/43-cycle clocks agree to 0.000019 d and yield a rounded blended-aperture photometric period 16.8905 d. Adjacent 43/42 and 45/44 count pairs miss the middle event by 0.1983 and 0.1902 d. The first four submultiples predict fully covered extra flat cores, with local losses near zero versus measured 3.6-4.1 ppt in the same window metric. The full-period S99 prediction lies inside the actual 4055.724-4063.034 gap, so it is not a null. Gaia P38.46865 d is incompatible with this photometric clock. The helper's fresh WCS TESSCut has valid target flux but a G13.015 neighbor 0.36 pixel away; its difference centroid and drift do not identify the source. PROBABLE retains real repeated blended flux eclipses while leaving target ownership and Gaia-orbit association unresolved. The numeric cycle, null and attribution receipts are in the target folder.
