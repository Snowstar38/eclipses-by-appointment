# Batch06 lead audit - T051-T060

The sealed `queue.csv` has ten exact targets and no substitutions. All ten bounded reviews are complete: eight REFUTED selected eclipse claims (T051-T056, T058, T060) and two retained PLAUSIBLE cases (T057, T059). Novelty is UNKNOWN for each because the shared VSX, ASAS-SN and Kostov 2025 tables 3/4 gates were unavailable despite positive-control attempts. Available primary-table nonmatches do not establish novelty. No case supplies a defensible measured eclipse period, primary epoch or eclipse depth, so these result fields are blank. Gaia periods are selection information.

`inspect_raw.py` verified every cached bulk packet and its individual QLP FITS SHA-256, then plotted the retained windows in raw SAP and an all-sector Gaia-period overview. `check_steps.py` measured actual local cadence gaps, quality-zero SAP and background medians. `predicted_windows.py` records sampled same/half-phase Gaia predictions with core and sideband cadence counts; predictions are not timings or automatic nulls. The raw representative panels were inspected visually. `current_sectors.py` queried the official TESSCut footprint for each coordinate: new coverage is T051 s108/s109, T052 s104-s107, T057 s99; other targets have no sector newer than their cached QLP. These newer sectors cannot rescue positively refuted older artifacts by themselves. T057 s99 target cutout was tested but has effectively zero target flux and is not a null.

Deciding cases:

- T051/T053: immediate downward SAP steps across respective 0.213/0.211-d gaps with rising SAP_BKG and slow recovery; no complete ingress/egress. The helper independently measured adjacent 0.2-d medians (`helper-audit.txt`).
- T052: apparent pair of deep s69 descents before interruption/sector end has large simultaneous SAP_BKG rises; first resumes in a step after the gap. Eight unrelated same-CCD targets do not share the declines. This is source-specific background/aperture evidence, not a detector-wide common-mode claim or an eclipse clock.
- T054: s17 dip lies on a rising shoulder into a gap; s84 brightening flags track substantial background increases. A heartbeat interpretation was not demonstrated.
- T055: the retained dip core has the same flux as its preceding baseline (0.99538 versus 0.99408), while the subsequent 1.05563 brightening manufactured the apparent deficit. Target-centered s24 cutout pixels show flat flux before the gap and a large positive rise after it. The dip is a baseline artifact; the brightening's origin is separate.
- T056: s99 target pixels show a broad pre-gap decline, gap step and later strong brightening, matching the QLP ramp/plateau rather than a localized eclipse. The retained 4068.53 window has no discrete event to center.
- T057: raw s9 dips have separations near multiples of the helper's 0.375302-d periodogram peak and a coherent depressed short-period fold. QLP corrected flux also retains a depression. A separate s9 TESSCut pixel extraction has positive target flux but strong sawtooth contamination and does not independently validate the shape; s99 cutout target flux is essentially zero. Source, eclipse kind and 0.3753/0.7506-d aliases remain unresolved. This is PLAUSIBLE short-period variability, not a demonstrated 32.95-d Gaia-orbit eclipse.
- T058: several sectors' retained dips and brightenings straddle orbit interruptions with step changes and broad ramps, not same-kind repeated eclipse profiles.
- T059: s25/s26 windows are smooth multi-day depressions; target-centered s25 TESSCut pixels also show a deeper decline toward sector end. This is not securely an eclipse, but a partial long-duration event cannot be refuted from the available shapes. Retained PLAUSIBLE, without a measured center or cycle label.
- T060: six QLP sectors independently show a roughly 1.03-d wave, with helper grid peaks 1.0297-1.0334 d. The broad retained flags sample continuous modulation, providing a positive non-eclipse alternative. The physical origin of the short wave is open.

The three target-centered deciding cutouts for T055/T056/T059 have positive target-pixel flux at their actual WCS positions; `inspect_deciders.py` records exact positions, hashes and 1x1/3x3/5x5 morphology. They use the same TESS observations as QLP, not an independent observing occasion. The T057 s9/s99 cutouts and the helper's numeric/plot artifacts are retained even where the attempted extraction was not decisive. Pixel difference maps are not calibrated source probabilities. No positive eclipse here justifies a Gaia orbit geometry claim; source attribution is unresolved for plausible cases.
