# Independent helper audit

Inputs: queue.csv; shared-catalogues/gaia-neighbors.json and its query receipt; source FITS identified and SHA-256 verified by helper-fetch.json and helper-F008-coverage.json. TESSCut files are alternative extractions of the same photons, not independent occasions. Screening centers and broad intervals are not measured timings. Pixel sky uses the outer two-pixel border; each cutout has finite positive central target sum and positive median sky pixels. Individual 3x3 measurements and coordinates are in helper-spatial.json; comparative Gaia positions, separations and G flux ratios are in helper-neighbor-wcs.json. Gaia G ratios are bandpass-dependent approximations, and flux-ratio/centroid comparisons are not by themselves source proofs.
