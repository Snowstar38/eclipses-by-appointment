# TIC 438089101 temperature and tide: bounded audit, 2026-11-05

The four-color calculation supports a cool, approximately 7,000–7,200 K photometric solution under its adopted dwarf-color and extinction model. The nearby-star extinction distribution is consistent with it. These are useful evidence against uncritical use of TIC's 11,991 K, but are not an independent spectroscopic temperature, a calibrated uncertainty, or proof that the TIC value is impossible. Preserve the heartbeat and independent oscillation results; keep beaming withdrawn on its aperture and sector instability.

## Numerical checks

Fable's published grid gives 7,025 K, E(B−V)=0.135, with profile Δχ²=1 ranges 6,700–7,675 K and 0.065–0.238. The stated “7,000 ± 350 K” does not represent that asymmetric interval. The 6,375–8,200 K Δχ²=4 interval is conditional on the same model, not a total error budget.

I reran the supplied interpolation/grid, preserving its per-color independent systematic floor but adding the positive covariance from the shared Ks error (0.023 mag) in G−Ks, V−Ks and J−Ks. At 0.02 mag floor, the minimum is 7,125 K, E(B−V)=0.155, χ²=0.803; profile Δχ²=1 is 6,775–7,700 K and 0.083–0.245. At an illustrative 0.04 mag floor, it is 7,125 K, 0.153, χ²=0.409, with 6,600–7,875 K and 0.045–0.268. This is a sensitivity exercise, not a derived systematic uncertainty or a calibration of correlated model errors. Script: sensitivity.py. The cool solution is not an artifact of ignoring the measured Ks covariance.

The color sequence is explicitly a dwarf sequence; applying it to an evolved primary, with mixed Gaia DR2/DR3 photometry and passbands and fixed broad-band extinction coefficients, introduces unquantified model systematics. A 0.02 mag independent floor is not evidence those effects are fully covered. Gaia-band extinction coefficients vary with source spectrum and extinction: see [Gaia G-band extinction coefficient](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201732327). Pecaut & Mamajek's [sequence](https://github.com/emamajek/SpectralType/blob/master/EEM_dwarf_UBVIJHK_colors_Teff.txt) is explicitly for dwarfs. Field-star distances and extinctions are jointly inferred from Gaia spectra/photometry and priors, with selection and systematic effects; the target-bin median E(B−V) ~0.14–0.15 is supportive population evidence, not an independent precise target extinction. See [Gaia DR3 GSP-Phot documentation](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Data_analysis/chap_cu8par/sec_cu8par_apsis/ssec_cu8par_apsis_gspphot.html) and [Gaia DR3 survey properties](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243940).

The hot-grid test is not valid beyond 10,700 K: np.interp clamps all intrinsic colors and bolometric corrections at the endpoint. Thus the nominal 12,000 K injection is actually the 10,700 K model and cannot validate recovery at 12,000 K. The pinned-E χ²=45 applies only to the truncated model; it cannot establish that no hotter solution fits. The rows above 10,700 K in solve_out are likewise extrapolations using clamped colors/bolometric correction and should not be interpreted physically. The TIC Schlegel flag and E=0.555 establish the adopted input and an over-dereddening hypothesis; the nearby population supports it, but does not establish the exact individual dust column or full causal reconstruction of TIC temperature. Gaia RVS template temperature is a template-selection clue, not an independent temperature measurement.

## Tide check

The supplied scaling reproduces the reference 4.44 ratio and gives 1.69 at M1=2.0, m2=0.516, a=16.29, R1=3.65, fixed geometry, coefficient, and dilution. At M1=1.9–2.2 with R1 fixed, the range is 1.58–1.92. Exact agreement at fixed other assumptions requires R1=4.35, 19% above 3.65; alternatively the equilibrium coefficient would need to rise by about 69%. Whether the combined coefficient, radius, dilution, and model uncertainties cover the residual has not been demonstrated. M1 ~2 is an assumed evolutionary estimate, not measured by the color fit, and m2 and a are conditional on it and the adopted inclination. The tide calculation demonstrates a reduction in tension, not its resolution. R≈3.65 and L≈29 are model-dependent illustrative values at the best fit; no independently justified 10% radius error or ±0.3 solar mass uncertainty was established here.

## Suggested manuscript wording

“Using Gaia, Tycho-derived V, and 2MASS colors with a dwarf intrinsic-color sequence and an assumed extinction law, we find a conditional photometric solution near Teff ≃ 7,100 K and E(B−V) ≃ 0.15; the formal profile interval is roughly 6,800–7,700 K before unquantified calibration and stellar-model systematics. The median extinction of nearby Gaia field stars at comparable inferred distances is consistent with this solution, although it need not equal the target's extinction. The TIC value of 11,991 K uses a substantially larger Schlegel-based reddening and should be treated cautiously. A spectrum or appropriately validated XP fit is needed to establish the temperature. For illustrative parameters M1=2.0 M☉ and R1=3.65 R☉, the fitted tidal scale is about 1.7 times the diluted equilibrium estimate, versus 4.4 using the TIC-based parameters; this comparison depends on the assumed stellar parameters and response coefficient.”

Remove “±350 K,” “supersedes,” the categorical exclusion of 12,000 K and claim of normal-coefficient agreement; qualify strip membership and primary mass as model-dependent. The 12.81–13.65 c/d group is consistent with δ Scuti-like pulsation if intrinsic to the target, while strip placement follows the adopted photometric solution. Do not reinstate beaming.
