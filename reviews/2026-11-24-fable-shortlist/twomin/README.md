# Twenty two-minute TESS leads from Fable’s shortlist

Fable 5.2 identified 610 lower-tier candidates with two-minute TESS light curves among the 707 original lower-tier targets. The review registers accounted for 255 of the 610. We selected 20 fields from the 355 remaining TICs for a fresh photometric check. One reaches the confirmed physical tier. Three others match previously published eclipsing sources, and a fourth position coincides with a previously published eclipsing binary at a different period. The other 15 have individual reasons for withholding an addition. [Ranked targets, scores, dispositions and evidence paths](screened.csv)

**TIC 305928109 (TYC 2754-995-1)** shows three individually measured primary eclipses. Their fitted period is 14.09235 days and the median aperture depth is 5.95 parts per thousand. The QLP and TESS-SPOC reductions both recover the three events. Raw pixel differences in sectors 56 and 83 place the dips on Gaia DR3 1886986498451472128. Gaia previously published a 14.09130-day single-lined spectroscopic orbit with eccentricity 0.445 and primary velocity semiamplitude 35.0 km/s. The observing baseline gives three well-covered eclipse times and no secure secondary. The TESS pipeline and Fable’s screening both predate these measurements. **TIC 9029822** has 28 measured fades across seven TESS sectors. The 4.72895-day spacing predicts faint observations in ZTF g on five nights, ASAS-SN V on ten and ASAS-SN g on thirteen. Fainter and brighter alternating events do not consistently separate by parity within each sector. Kounkel et al. already listed the APOGEE double-lined system 2M23423710-1059312 as a detached eclipsing binary with a 4.737-day TESS period in 2021. We retain the measurements as a recovery. ## Sampling and measurements

We required at least three original significant events in at least two sectors, a measured depth of at least 3 parts per thousand, a trial period between 0.35 and 40 days and a positive duration below 14% of the period. We screened the current catalogue, both IJspeert eclipse tables and the Prša catalogue by exact TIC and within 30 arcseconds. Fifty-five fields survived this first selection. We scored them from the original two-minute measurements. A baseline amplitude below 40% of the primary depth added six points, duty below half-depth at most 14% added five, and a secondary at signal-to-noise above six and above 10% of the primary added four. The absence of a flux-capable neighbour within 42 arcseconds added three, seven or more individual primary detections added two, and three or more sectors added one. We chose the top 20, breaking ties by descending Fable rank score and ascending TIC. Additional checks against the wider field and SuperWASP applied after rank selection.

We retrieved 87 QLP and TESS-SPOC full-frame light-curve products spanning the first, middle and last available validation sectors per target. Product availability varies. The field records contain product URLs and hashes, folded flux, each trial clock and the sector-specific event lists. Each event entry contains the integer cycle, predicted time in BTJD, measured depth and adjacent scatter in parts per thousand, and the event flag. We required three cadences in the inner window and in each out-of-event shoulder. The flag requires a positive depth greater than both 1.5 parts per thousand and three times the local robust scatter. These two reductions use shared spacecraft exposures. Ten targets received a subsequent two-sector source comparison with original SPOC target-pixel FITS and the Gaia source scene. A sector with insufficient data carries its own outcome.

The catalogue comparison included 11 controlled, 30-arcsecond VizieR queries. We checked the strongest leads by aliases and source-specific tables. A positive-control source was recovered in each batch. The source records retain narrower survey, bibliography and prior-discovery limits. A weak recovery or ambiguous host disposition applies to the tested period and data products. [Catalogue checks and matches](controlled-catalogues.json)
