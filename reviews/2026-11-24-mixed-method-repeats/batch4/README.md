# Batch 4 repeat review

Ten targets were reviewed using 77 original TESS QLP target-sector light curves. No target showed a repeatable discrete flux loss that survived the timing, morphology and field comparison checks.

The clock trials produced 70,572 predictions. Of these, 4,998 meet the primary window coverage requirement, 1,419 fall within sector spans with insufficient core or shoulder coverage, and 64,155 are outside the loaded sector spans. These counts are clock-window combinations. Several clocks can predict the same observed event. The [coverage table](coverage-summary.csv) shows results by target and clock at minimum coverage levels of 55%, 80%, and 95%. The ten [outcomes](outcomes.csv) preserve the input order.

Individual samples across the two S35 structures go down by about 5–6 ppt, with nearby irregular lows throughout the same couple of days. At both exposure neighborhoods, two controls on other chips show contemporaneous depressions while the chip medians of 185 field stars are near zero. The trial 1.148 d spacing between the visually selected event centers predicts 115 measurable windows, 114 of them at or below 0.75 ppt in either product. At 80% coverage, all 100 measurable windows are weak. Tested subdivisions of the spacing also produce many well-observed empty windows. - **TIC 387410061 and Batch 1 TIC 137203172, S85.** A short loss in TIC 387410061 (camera 3 CCD 4, BTJD 3629.30861, 0.04 d, 3.60 ppt corrected and 3.65 ppt raw) overlaps the isolated short loss of TIC 137203172 (camera 3 CCD 2, BTJD 3629.32883). The best supported 1.1597111 d modulation clock shows a smooth double wave over 22 sectors. This clock leaves 250 of 385 measurable predictions at or below 0.75 ppt in either product. At 80% coverage, none of the 352 measurable predictions exceeds 2 ppt in both products. The S22 disturbance remains isolated in these data. ## Reproduction

Run `python review.py` with Python, NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. The script accepts `--fits-dir`, `--prior-dir` and `--mask`. Run `python camera_checks.py` for the field comparisons, with an optional `--fits-dir` override. The scripts use the original FITS cadence IDs, BTJD, QUALITY=0, and finite SAP and corrected data. The sector-26 guard joins on sector, camera, corrected flux column and original CADENCENO. Batch 4 includes no sector-26 target curves, so zero cadences match the calibrated guard. The S22, S35, S77 and S85 camera plots are targeted diagnostic comparisons distinct from the calibrated sector-26 mask.

The box-least-squares stage uses corrected flux divided by a moving median continuum, sector-specific weights and outlier clipping. Trials include the Gaia period with free phase, its half and double, a coarse 1.5–65 d search, early and late short-period scans, and fine searches near selected alternates. The BLS score ranks trial clocks. The coarse grid can skip peaks narrow in period over the full baseline. Depths use unbinned raw and corrected data relative to a line through the shoulder medians. A valid measurement needs at least two samples in each of the core and shoulders and at least 55% of the expected nominal sampling in each region. Positive depth indicates loss. The checks are based on aperture photometry.

- [files.csv](files.csv) lists original target-sector inputs, observing spans, camera, CCD and mask counts.
- [events.csv](events.csv) measures all previously flagged dip windows after applying the guard.
- [clocks.csv](clocks.csv) and [predictions.csv](predictions.csv) give tested ephemerides, cycle coverage and depth measurements.
- [short-search.csv](short-search.csv) records the independent short-window search for TICs 24533832, 45184585, 387410061 and 148254196.
- [camera-comparisons.csv](camera-comparisons.csv) lists field-star depth measurements by detector and exposure neighborhood.
- [plots](plots/) contains original-cadence event panels, sector phase plots and field comparisons. Neighboring masked or insufficiently sampled windows remain visible in individual panels where points exist.
