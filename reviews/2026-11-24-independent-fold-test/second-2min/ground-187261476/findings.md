The light curves do not yield a secure independent confirmation of the ~30 ppt TESS dips under this limited check. Fold plots are included.

Several are 2-3 mag faint, despite small quoted errors. This strongly camera-dependent distribution raises a quality/systematic concern; it does not establish that every point is instrumental. No multi-cadence coherent deep episode was established, so isolated faint points do not confirm other deep events. A larger 4-arcsec cone and source crowding also limit origin attribution.
