# Independent helper audit: T081-T090

All ten screened peak windows are independently extracted from TESSCut with scripts helper_fetch.py, helper_fetch_remaining.py and helper_pixels.py; event centers are retained detector windows, not precision fits. FITS and download receipts (including hashes) live within target folders, and helper-pixel-audit.json records measurements and the shared neighbor-catalog hash. Re-run extraction via python helper_pixels.py. This is a different extraction of the same photons, not another observing occasion. Finite positive median target flux and 51-54 positive-median sky pixels were present in every reviewed frame; none had zero median pixels. Background uses positive pixels more than 3 pixels from target, not the lowest-pixel estimator. Three to 37 quality-zero core samples; positive baseline in all. Plots show the specific local event, not full phase or cycle coverage.

T081, T082, T083, T087, T088, T089 and T090 show deep target-region difference flux and eclipse-like local shapes. T084 is bright and saturated: the split difference across its bleed renders brightest-pixel offsets unreliable. T086 has a centered difference. These measurements do not by themselves establish which system or orbital period causes the event. T085 has two Gaia neighbors only 17.14 and 17.88 arcsec away at (5.24,4.76)/(5.28,4.77), while the target is (4.44,4.62). The 5x5 signed flux-difference centroid is (5.113,4.825), leaning toward the neighbor pair; it is not PRF-fitted and has no statistical uncertainty, so attribution is unresolved. helper_moments.py and helper-moments.json preserve this calculation. Gaia G flux ratios are approximate, and their comparison with blended aperture depth is only a candidate screen, not an exclusion bound. Outside-aperture neighbors require actual response geometry; faint unresolved sources or catalog incompleteness also limit attribution.

T088 has an additional independent observing occasion in sector 77 near BTJD 3422.485, with a real 30 ppt eclipse-like sky-corrected aperture dip (helper-s77-difference.png and helper-s77-pixel-audit.json). A Gaia neighbor at 12.16 arcsec has approximate G flux ratio 0.04 and may be capable of producing it, so attribution is not exclusive. The sector 50 event is about 219 ppt; the two are at different Gaia phases, so they are not evidence of a same-kind period by themselves. No eclipse model or timing fit is claimed.
