# Faint-band conjunction and raw-event search

A frozen, stratified hash selection of 100 previously unreviewed Gaia spectroscopic binaries at G11.5–12 and P20–60 d yielded 100 resolved TIC identities and usable QLP products. All selected objects, scores and screening outcomes remain in the archive. Two historical known-EB controls are separate. This is an exploratory combined conjunction-window and raw-event search; visual triage selected nine for full bounded review.

The nine reviews yield two confirmed, three probable, two plausible and two refuted selected signals. One probable is a known recovery, leaving four accepted claims: R771 and R774 probable; R772 and R773 confirmed. The current total is 967 (828 confirmed, 139 probable). Two apparent repeat signals are spatially identified as neighboring EBs. The raw repeated-event yield cannot be equated to new-system yield.

All 18 target VSX/ASAS-SN cones succeeded, bracketed by same-night known-positive controls; full primary-table and Kostov checks, Gaia, SIMBAD, TOI, 3,584 included KEBC entries and a bounded 244-eprint corpus were checked. KEBC scope excludes disabled campaigns and non-included candidates; literature scope is not exhaustive priority proof. See exact query receipts in shared-catalogues. Physical limitations remain in each report.
