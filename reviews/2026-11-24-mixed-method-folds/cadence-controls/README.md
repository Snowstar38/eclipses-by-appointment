# Exposure-level camera check

Two unrelated targets on different chips shared exposure 38482. We used their integer cadence number to check the same exposure in other sky positions, together with the camera 2 event on 38490. The field control draw used the original sector 26 QLP FITS collection (447 files), a salted filename hash and a cap of 12 field stars per chip. Neither the star selection nor the comparison baseline used corrected flux to pick stars. The ten review targets were ineligible for the control group. We used the three triggers separately from the 192 comparison stars. [Registered field roster, archive URLs and hashes](roster.csv)

For a given exposure, a star must have quality zero, both flux columns finite, and at least two good measurements in each of the four preceding and four following exposure numbers. The baseline is the average of the two flanking medians for that flux column. A flag requires a corrected loss greater than 5% of baseline with absolute aperture-flux change less than 1%. A star absent on the exposure or without both flanks contributes no vote. Twenty comparison exposures are spaced sixteen cadences apart on either side of 38482.

A subsequent sector-wide search applied the same flux rule to the 192 field stars at all available exposure numbers. A camera/exposure requires 12 usable stars, at least two chips with three or more measured stars, and 80% of measured stars passing. These thresholds were chosen after examining 38482 and 38490. The third timestamp emerged from the full-sector scan. Changes in cadence, extraction pipeline and camera coverage require validation before reusing the numerical thresholds elsewhere. [Field-star measurements](measurements.csv) · [All camera/exposure counts](exposure-scan.csv)

For another light curve, join the sector, camera, QLP flux column and original `CADENCENO` to the three-row list. A match flags that measurement when assessing a fold. Review the raw aperture and any other observed eclipse before classifying the star. [Three-row lookup](flagged-exposures.csv)

Run `python check.py CACHE` to fetch the listed FITS or reuse a local copy, validate all hashes and regenerate the comparison measurements. Run `python scan.py CACHE` and `python plot.py CACHE` to reproduce the full camera scan and plot. The optional `--draw` requires the original full 447-product selection pool. The frozen roster suffices for replay.
