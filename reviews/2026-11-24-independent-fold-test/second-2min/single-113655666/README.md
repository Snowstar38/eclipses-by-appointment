# TIC 113655666 background check

The isolated sector 43 dip near BTJD 2491.279732 tracks the SPOC background estimate. In the optimal target-pixel aperture, the median background-subtracted flux falls by 674 electrons/s (10.5%) while the estimated background rises by 716 electrons/s. Adding the model back gives a 38 electrons/s increase (0.46%). The independent sum of raw detector counts increases by 0.026%. The apparent eclipse depth is explained by the background subtraction.

The measurement uses quality-zero observations within ±0.010 days of the event center. The baseline combines observations 0.15 to 0.30 days away on both sides.
