# Batch 14 | Dip review

All three S48 TIC 219846230 dips originate in one two-state detector pixel. Its cutout indices are zero-based (x=6, y=9), corresponding to TESS camera 4 CCD 2 physical column 162, row 1090. The native calibrated losses are 55.91, 59.89 and 58.72 electrons/s at 2616.2194, 2625.9347 and 2627.3097 BTJD after local background subtraction. Summed changes of the eight adjacent pixels are -0.21, -0.82 and -3.33 electrons/s in loss units. Nearby empirical point profiles have adjacent-to-central flux ratios of 2.06 to 2.66. Those ratios would predict approximately 115 to 160 electrons/s in the adjacent pixels for the measured center losses. Matched blank-window scatter instead limits adjacent losses to about 8 to 12% of the central loss. The levels repeat before and after the highlighted windows, with intermediate transition samples and QLP correlation of 0.965 to 0.980. This points to unstable pixel response with random-telegraph-like switching. The underlying hardware mechanism was not measured. The pixel audit fits a planar background to faint pixels per FFI exposure because the FITS FLUX_BKG column contains zero throughout. It estimates each pixel's event loss against an interpolated baseline from equal-width flanks. Local pseudo-events between 0.25 and 1.1 d away supply a robust adjacent-sum scatter, after excluding windows near the three events. The conservative limit is the measured positive part plus three times the blank-window MAD scaled to standard-deviation units. It is an empirical limit, not a formal probability bound. The TESScut timestamps are matched to QLP within 17.28 seconds, as TESScut stores zero for every cadence ID. Reproduce from the cached FITS with python -B pixel_219846230.py or pass paths with --ffi and --qlp. ## Review files

- [Outcomes](outcomes.csv) lists all ten dispositions. Periods of smooth variables describe photometric modulation. Epochs for isolated events describe observing windows.
- [Original event measurements](archive-windows.csv), [covered predictions](predicted-windows.csv) and [sector coverage](sector-coverage.csv) use quality-zero QLP flux, with KSPSAP_FLUX or DET_FLUX as corrected flux. Signed depth is positive for a dip. Side-window medians define the local linear baseline. Coverage summaries require at least 65% core coverage and two cadences per side. Internal gaps are retained in the prediction table.
- The [independent scan](independent-scan.csv), [per-sector short periods](periodograms.csv), [background](background.csv) and [camera controls](camera-controls.csv) record supporting evidence. Controls compare cadence numbers within the same sector and camera. In control figures, gray lines are same-CCD SAP and the lower panel is target SAP_BKG. The roster is limited to cached QLP targets.
- The two visual-guide JPGs summarize the rendered and reviewed event panels. Individual sector plots mark Gaia predictions as dashed purple lines. Selected fold and time-series panels provide additional shape detail.
- Shared source is in [batch 11](../batch11). From the project directory run python -B batch11/review.py --batch batchNN, then python -B batch11/independent.py --batch batchNN and python -B batch11/controls.py --batch batchNN. The fold.py and refine_dips.py scripts regenerate folded diagnostics and short-dip coverage. Dependencies are NumPy, Astropy, SciPy and Matplotlib.
