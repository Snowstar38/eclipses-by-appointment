# Period-free screening and complementary leads: 250 reviews

The [review register](../../period-free-review.csv) links each target to its deciding report and records the physical outcome, novelty status, measurements, limitations, and next check. Campaign and sample identifiers record how work was organized; they are not scientific classifications or ranks. The review combined an initial stratified 100 with 150 subsequently prioritized targets. The outcome-informed selection and unequal sampling mean these fractions are neither population occurrence rates nor independent validation of the ranking.

Novelty is separate: 206 unknown, 9 known, and 35 scoped nonmatches. Among confirmed and probable cases, 6 are known recoveries, 41 have unknown novelty, and 8 scoped nonmatches passed claim review (one confirmed, seven probable; R725-R732). A failed or unavailable catalogue query is not a nonmatch; a bounded nonmatch is not proof of novelty. **Eight reviewed systems enter the current accepted claim total; 41 remain catalogue-pending.** Refutation concerns the reviewed signal or attribution, not every possible binary interpretation. Probable and plausible cases retain the limitations in their reports.

The mixed harvest combined dip and brightening detections with period-fold-only and near-threshold fold leads. The first 100 included 30 cross-method dip, 20 other multi-sector dip, 20 single-sector dip, 10 fold-only, 10 near-threshold fold and 10 brightening targets; the subsequent 150 prioritized dip leads. These detector thresholds apply to dip screening, not uniformly to the mixed harvest. Dip screening used quality-zero, positive finite SAP flux, ten-minute bins where needed, six trial durations from 0.06 to 1.6 days, at least 0.001 fractional depth and contrast of at least five. Maximized contrast is a screening statistic, not a calibrated detection probability. The inclusive harvest had 2,984 leads, 2,904 after 80 historical identity exclusions, and 2,654 remained outside these reviews. Independent reductions of the same exposures are not independent observing occasions; missing coverage is not a null detection.

The campaign directories retain compact reports, numeric measurements, figures, receipts and scripts where available. Relative references in historical reports may point to omitted bulk data or local caches; the export does not contain raw FITS, archives, or every external catalogue response, and therefore cannot guarantee a complete offline rerun. The reports identify further observational and catalogue limitations. [Provenance hashes](provenance-sha256.csv) give the SHA-256 of each original file and its public export; text has been sanitized, so the hashes can differ. Source files remain unchanged. Scripts may require the original cached data and environment, and local paths in exported text have been redacted.

The [selection provenance](selection/README.md) retains all 6,186 screened targets, their scores and identities, the 80 exclusions, the 2,804-target priority queue, and the exact first 150 reviewed priorities.

Other hash lists and hashes embedded in historical receipts describe original source files, not necessarily the sanitized public copies; use the provenance table to verify exported bytes.
