# Batch 06

The isolated SAP dips of TIC 341293943 are the strongest unresolved long-period lead here. At the 38.8686 d trial, well-covered S13, S27 and S101 windows have localized troughs, including the previously unflagged S27 one. A robust Gaussian-plus-linear baseline fit at five selected windows has depths about 2.5–9.0 ppt, approximate widths 0.89–1.56 d and timing residuals from −0.288 to +0.243 d. S102 and S104 sit on broad gap-affected trends. The corrected flux is nearly flat at the corresponding times. The fit values are descriptive. The target is catalogued as an ASAS MISC variable at 29.69466 d. Independent central-aperture fits depend on baseline and do not localize this tentative repeat. [Pixel fits](../followup/341293943.png) · [Catalog evidence](../credit/group2/wide.json) The separate larger S101 SAP decline around 4112.39 BTJD is shared with other stars.

TIC 128781401 has three SAP troughs near 14 Peg heartbeat maxima at 2831.43, 2847.34 and 3579.39 BTJD. The nearest common spacecraft cadences are 692913, 699788 and 1016032. The stars both use camera 2, with 128781401 on CCD 4 and 14 Peg on CCD 3. Several other 14 Peg peaks have no matching deep negative pulse, excluding regular all-cycle negative replication. The three aligned dips share one residue of the 5.30469 d pulse clock. A trial using that residue every third cycle puts the only S16 opportunity in missing data, and S83 at 3563.476 is covered but has its local dip about 0.6 d early. The local fits have changing depth, shape and timing. Independent images at the strong S56 2847 and S83 3579 events favor the catalogued YSO Gaia DR3 1904993269100090624 about 40.6 arcsec away. The S56 2831 fit is baseline-sensitive. The timing comparisons do not establish an instrumental association with 14 Peg. The recurrence clock remains open. [Pixel fits](../followup/128781401.png) · [Measurements](../followup/measurements.csv)

TIC 409755474 retains a two-event hypothesis with sparse useful coverage and temporal proximity to a separate S102 target's disturbance. TIC 214689049 is a strong continuous ~0.13835 d variable in all five sectors. Most other flagged broad events in this group are shared depressions or trending flux next to gaps. The period trials in the evidence record covered quiet opportunities where available.
