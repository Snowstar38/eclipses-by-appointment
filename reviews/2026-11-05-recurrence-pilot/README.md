# Prospective recurrence-versus-contrast pilot

Twenty previously unreviewed multi-sector dip leads received complete bounded physical reviews, blinded to selection arm: ten selected by a frozen recurrence score and ten by original contrast from the remaining pool. Both arms yielded zero confirmed or probable target eclipsers. There were 18 selected-signal refutations and two plausible cases; none adds a claim. The arms were not randomized treatments, so this is a descriptive operational comparison, not a causal test. Recurrence scoring did not demonstrate improved yield.

The cumulative mixed-method register now contains 370 full reviews: 28 confirmed, 36 probable, 67 plausible, 229 selected-signal refutations, five non-eclipse variables and five unresolved. Accepted claims remain 963 (826 confirmed, 137 probable). Physical and novelty classifications remain separate.
