# Source attribution in two spectroscopic-target fields

TIC 423332688 is a probable eclipsing binary 18 arcsec from the original aperture target. The two broad minima also permit a rotational or ellipsoidal interpretation. A smooth two-harmonic model explains 87.8 to 88.7 percent of the held-out phase-bin variance in each TESS sector. Four harmonics explain 96.0 to 97.0 percent. The available spectra do not establish an orbit for this source. A fit to 256 primary event times in TESS sectors 62, 89 and 99 gives 0.226832194 d. At half that period, successive minima alternate in depth. Both minima localize to TIC 423332688 in each sector. Spatially resolved ZTF light curves place the source within 0.1 arcsec of the Gaia position in both filters. The large difference between the TESS and ZTF fractional depths follows from dilution in the TESS aperture. The CSV depth is the median diluted depth measured in individual TESS events. Its ZTF primary depth is about 21–23 percent. Gaia, VSX and ATLAS previously reported variability at this position.

The TIC 453588386 field contains a separate repeating pair of eclipses at 0.964733 d. Two sectors favour TIC 453588388. Sector 65 favours a neighbouring Gaia star for both minima, and a third nearby star is unresolved. The host count excludes this field.

The source records contain the independent ephemeris fits, timed events, difference-image comparisons, astrometric neighbours, catalogue matches, and target-specific bibliography searches. Barycentric times use BJD TDB minus 2457000. The quoted regression errors describe statistical uncertainty. Inputs are from [MAST TESScut](https://mast.stsci.edu/tesscut/), [ZTF at IRSA](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/ztf.html), [Gaia DR3](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/), [HEASARC VSX](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/W3Browse/all/aavsovsx.html), [ATLAS variable stars](https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/VizieR?-source=J/AJ/156/241/table4), and [NASA ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/).
