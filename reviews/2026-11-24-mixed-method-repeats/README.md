# Sixty mixed-method leads revisited

Six ten-target reviews examined **60 distinct TESS apertures in 454 original QLP target-sector light curves**. Three apertures have well-supported repeating dimming. The brightening search independently recovered the published heartbeat of 14 Pegasi. A deep eclipse in a fifth aperture traces to a neighbor already reviewed by this project. These results add zero new systems to the accepted catalogue. Each target retains its original disposition and any subsequent cross-check. ## What changed after cross-checking

- **14 Pegasi (TIC 287857379).** Searching the brightenings recovered a pulse every 5.30468583 d, within 3.1 seconds of Petrie's published spectroscopic period. An early-sector fit predicts the later clean pulse peaks within 0.010 d. All 14 well-covered predicted pulses brighten, with roughly 1% peak amplitude. The original TESS study identifies this as a heartbeat binary, in which tidal distortion changes the light near closest approach. Six other leads in this batch have flare-like shapes, two have other short-period variability, and one shows instrumental trends. [Four sectors of pulses](batch5/plots/287857379-pulse-train.png) · [Published heartbeat analysis and historical spectroscopy](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202039553)
- **TIC 212431508 and its neighbor.** Two sector 81 dips are deeper in the aperture of TIC 212431520, 35.7 arcsec away. The deeper event's lost light falls 0.59 arcsec from that neighbor and 36.24 arcsec from the nominated target. Baselines from opposite sides move the fit by 0.11 arcsec. The shallower event has insufficient before-event coverage in the quality-selected TESS-SPOC images. The neighbor already has project review T092 and a Kostov machine flag. This recovers the existing primary through spillover. The two unequal events still leave the orbital period open. [Paired light curves](batch6/212431508-212431520-eclipse-pair.png) · [Deeper-event image](pixels/212431508.png) · [Original T092 review](https://github.com/Snowstar38/eclipses-by-appointment/blob/main/reviews/2026-11-05-period-free/binary-priority100-2026-11-05/batch10/212431520/REPORT.md)
- **Coincident isolated dips.** TICs 137203172 and 387410061 are on different chips of the same camera. Their losses begin at nearly the same sector 85 exposure. Two of 100 additional field stars also dip in both flux products, despite nearly flat chip medians. This weakens the isolated-event interpretation and illustrates why checks of individual comparison stars matter. In sector 94 a separate loss is shared by 36 of 37 sampled camera peers. [Sector 85 traces](batch4/plots/camera-S85-1037602.png) · [Sector 94 measurements](batch6/README.md)
- **Ground survey.** For the first 30 targets, the ASAS-SN search returned usable data for 28 and ZTF for one. At the historical QLP epoch and Gaia period, 15 targets had adequate comparisons and yielded no recovery. The other 15 had insufficient coverage. None of the three fitted dimming clocks passed the fixed ground-survey criteria. A separate search allowed the early ground data to choose the phase. One of 39 held-out series passed its numerical cut, TIC 253463221 in ASAS-SN g, with a 0.83-mag median loss in seven late cycles. At five well-covered predictions the TESS SAP depth ranges from −0.49 to +1.19 ppt. The large ground-only signal lacks TESS corroboration. ## Selection and evidence

The November 5 mixed-method harvest contained 2,904 targets. The selected TICs exclude the 380 previously recorded mixed-method reviews, the subsequent ten fold-only reviews, the existing scientific register and accepted hosts, and the active parallel lists checked at reservation. Positional matching used 30 arcsec where coordinates were available. A signal from outside that radius can enter the nominated aperture, as the 35.7 arcsec T092 example demonstrates. Selection used historical flags and visual rankings. The yield describes this selected sample.

## Reproducing measurements

From this review directory, `python inputs/prepare.py 5` retrieves and verifies the 59 QLP products for batch 5. Then run `python batch5/review_brightenings.py`. Substitute the batch number and script from the relevant review for other batches. Add `--controls` to the preparation command for the batch 4 or 6 same-camera comparisons and the batch 6 neighboring aperture. The preparer writes an input workspace under `inputs/workspace`. Each historical screen retains its event windows, sector and original FITS digest. The manifest also lists the comparison stars. Python dependencies for the light curves are Requests, NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. The ground scripts additionally use pandas and the ASAS-SN Sky Patrol client. [Input manifest](inputs/qlp.csv) · [MIT Quick-Look Pipeline](https://tess.mit.edu/qlp/)

The `pixels` scripts use public TESS-SPOC target-pixel products, TESScut full-frame cutouts and Gaia DR3 fields. Run `python pixels/retrieve.py CACHE` and then `python pixels/measure.py CACHE` for the single-event images. Use `python pixels/spoc_curve.py CACHE` for the three independent reductions and `python pixels/stack.py SEPARATE_FFI_CACHE` for the short-period stacks. Input locations and hashes are recorded with the measurements. These are measurements of the same TESS exposures. ASAS-SN and ZTF are independent ground instruments.
