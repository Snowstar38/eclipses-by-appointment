# Batch 07

TIC 240799649 displays closely recurring minima in both SAP and corrected curves across S57, S58, S84 and S85. A short-period trial refined by comparing sector-fold primary minima gives approximately 0.456000 d and 3600.98596 BTJD, with distinct shallow secondary and two broad maxima. The 0.06 d width in the outcome is the typical archive window, not a fitted contact duration. The two minima in each orbital cycle explain the strong half-period spectral peak near 0.228 d. Independent pixel measurements place the eclipses within about 1 arcsec of Gaia DR3 415033586148701056, about 63.8 arcsec from the TIC. This is the catalogued EW binary WISE J004852.7+510946 with a Gaia period of 0.4559981 d. The batch recovered a known neighboring eclipsing binary in the QLP aperture. TIC 121999831 has strong smooth ~0.798 d structure in SAP and a prominent ~0.3991 d corrected harmonic. Multiple sectors show this behavior. The sector-relative folds give broad minima and no narrow isolated eclipse at the target Gaia period. TIC 235594109 retains an uncertain sequence of low-amplitude SAP features near a 21.04 d trial, with selected center residuals up to 0.44 d and several partial opportunities. Other objects show short-timescale continuous variation, shared depressions or gap-side ramps.
