Screening only. Inspected all 100 science targets and both labeled historical controls in the 13 raw-SAP sheets. The candidate windows are selections, not fitted eclipses; times and Gaia periods are not adopted ephemerides. Every successful download was checked against its saved SHA-256 receipt (474 files), and file counts matched all 102 summary rows. The 9-object queue has no TIC in the accepted ledger. A queue entry invites full source attribution, recurrence and timing review; it is not a C/P verdict or novelty claim.
