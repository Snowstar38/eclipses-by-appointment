Reviewer: automated GPT-6.1 Terra, supervised by Astra. Coordinator: the project coordinator. Scope: the ten targets in targets-selected.json. No novelty catalogue queries performed. Examined all-raw.png, 18 QLP versus independent target-aperture comparisons (terra-independent-comparison.png), source comparisons and their scripts, and fitted fixed QLP-shaped templates to the independent target aperture (terra_measure.py, terra-helper-results.json). The independent extraction is the same FFI photons with a separately constructed aperture/background, not an independent observation; event shapes are fixed from QLP, and formal errors are uninflated. Differences in dilution and background make measured depths aperture-dependent. Existing fixed Gaussian SSE ratios are descriptive, not likelihoods; this review does not claim calibrated PRFs or source probabilities.
