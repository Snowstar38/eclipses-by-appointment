# Independent helper audit — batch 01

Scope: independent numerical re-fit of measured midpoints in `measurements.json`, inventory of historical difference-image measurements and capable Gaia neighbours, and visual inspection of the 253126207 image. The machine-readable `helper/audit-inventory.json` contains SHA-256 hashes for every timing JSON, sampled raw-window CSV, historical difference JSON, and cutout, plus numerical checks and neighbour IDs. Reproduction: `python helper/audit.py`. This is an audit of saved measurements, not an independent re-extraction of pixels or a calibrated centroid uncertainty. The historical image windows use ephemeris-selected centers; those numbers are not measured eclipse times. Difference-image centroids are measured pixel products, not predicted positions. A centroid close to a target does not by itself exclude a sub-pixel neighbour. Capability flags are approximate G-band flux-budget screens, not localization proofs.

Weighted linear slopes independently computed from free-fit midpoint times reproduce all ten reported periods within 9e-14 d. This checks arithmetic, not astrophysical uncertainty or cycle ambiguity. Raw windows and fits share the QLP source photons; alternative reductions are not independent observations. Half-cadence timing floors and inflated covariance are explicitly approximate and do not confer calibrated coverage.
