# Independent helper audit (TIC 412639100)

Reviewed the corrected QLP SAP_FLUX quality-zero raw event and stacked panels, the lead's analyze_sap.py and sap-measurements.json, and independently calculated local fixed-window depths with helper-local-depth.py. The lead's 10 cycles 0,1,11,53,54,55,79,80,89,90 are predicted windows, not independently measured eclipse times. Independent mean-box depths 66-278 ppm (median 131 ppm) agree in scale with the lead's median-box results 58-303 ppm (median 120 ppm); differences reflect mean vs median and local shoulder choices. The independent heuristic 0.1-day-bin red-noise scales are 40-98 ppm per event, not formal calibrated uncertainties. Positive deficits alone do not establish sharp eclipse morphology. The stack has a broad roughly day-long trough with no clear ingress or egress and is sensitive to baseline choice. Proximity to periastron allows a heartbeat-like interpretation but does not demonstrate it; eclipse and non-eclipse alternatives remain unresolved. Do not interpret the fixed-window depth as a measured central eclipse depth or the optimized minima as secure event timings.
